# older-neighbour-woman
Older neighbour who coughs and complains of coal dust. Older; exact age unspecified. Slow and annoyed, low energy.
Pitch: +2 semitones and formant change (starting point, test it). Unlike dulmaa: more irritated. Cues: 1.
References: EP01-VO-056: Нөгөө нүүрс нь тоос л байна. Өчигдрөөс хойш хоолой хорсоод.
