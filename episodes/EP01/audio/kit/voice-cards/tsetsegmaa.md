# tsetsegmaa

Ganaa’s wife and Altan’s mother.
She protects the family.

- Age: 30
- Rhythm and energy: Even and practical; controlled fear brings clipped urgency.
- Pitch: +4 to +7 semitones, formant ratio about 1.1, or female-target conversion (starting point, test it).
- Unlike dulmaa: quicker and more precise.
- Cues: 29
