# First voice test — 10 minutes

Do this before the animation is finished. These are source cues from the birthday sequence; the overlay's timing is a performance guide, not a replacement for cue text. Record each cue separately, then make a 15–20 second sample per character by joining takes with natural pauses; repeat a line if needed. Do not force the timing.

**0–2 min:** Set up as in RECORDING_GUIDE.md and capture 10 seconds of room tone.

**2–5 min: Ganaa.** Warm and a little tired, at child height; smile slightly. Record:

- EP01-VO-175: Чамд. Өмсөөд үз.
- EP01-VO-177: Жаахан том юм байна. Дараа жил бас өмсөнө.
- EP01-VO-182: Аав нь ер нь зөв дуулж байсан бил үү?

**5–7 min: Tsetsegmaa.** Practical and controlled, with relief and contained anger. Record:

- EP01-VO-153: Ганаа! Хааччихсан юм бэ? Алтан чинь өдөржин хаалга харлаа.
- EP01-VO-162: Юу гээд байгаа юм? Халуун чинь их байна ш дээ. Нааш ир, суугаад өг.
- EP01-VO-187: Тунгаа, түр хүлээ.

**7–9 min:** On copies in Audacity, try Ganaa at −1 to −2 semitones with EQ, and Tsetsegmaa at +4 to +7 semitones. In Praat, try Change Gender on Tsetsegmaa with formant ratio about 1.1; compare small adjustments. These are starting points, not final settings. Keep raw files private.

**9–10 min:** Compare the processed versions for naturalness, clear Mongolian pronunciation and audible artefacts. Can you hear two distinct people? Arrange a blind test with someone who knows your voice: ask whether they recognise you, without revealing which clip is yours. None of these tools guarantees anonymity. Keep the most promising settings and test conversion later if needed.
