# ХАНИАД — THE COUGH
## EP01 · АЯГАА ХУВААЛЦ
### v5.1 · full production script · dialogue, humour & period pass · target 26:00

**Status:** v5.1 is a dialogue, humour and period pass on v5 (`EP01_v5_FULL_SCRIPT.md`, untouched). Scene structure, beats, timecodes, story and hard facts are v5's. Every changed line is listed old → new, with an English gloss, in `EP01_v5.1_DIALOGUE_PASS.md`, together with the voice cards, the curse-density table, the death/likeability check and the money/period notes. Research sources are in `research-1998-notes.md`. `EP01_v5_CHANGES.md`, `EP01_v5_BEATS.csv` and `EP01_v5_NEW_ASSETS.md` still apply, except that Irina's **$20 is now 100 roubles** and the customs bribe is **one 5,000₮ note** (V07, V09).

**Form:** Action in short English panel lines (`▸` = one composed frame or a held move inside one frame). Spoken Mongolian in Cyrillic. Russian is spoken in Russian with `[хадмал: ...]`. Every spoken line ends with its id: `EP01-VO-xxx` = the v4.3 recording-manifest id (kept or edited line), `EP01-V5-xxx` = new in v5, `EP01-V51-xxx` = new in v5.1. An edited line keeps its id. Ids are never reused for a different line.

**Implied-horror rule (YouTube-safe):** no infant body, no feeding, and no open wound held on screen, ever. Horror comes from sound that stops, shadows, doors, objects left behind, and what people say afterwards. Blood is limited to soaked cloth, a thumbprint, or dark spots on gravel seen at a distance.

**Hard facts held in this script:** Ganaa's bite is on his **LEFT wrist**. The kitchen door opens **inward** and has **no lock**. The infected only **push or slam** doors and never use a handle. Naran has no traffic lights. There is no pharmacy scene. The enamel bowl is kindness and **not** how the infection spreads. Altan is a **3-year-old girl**. The protected line is in V18, word for word.

**Voice anchors (v5.1; full voice cards in `EP01_v5.1_DIALOGUE_PASS.md`):**
- **ГАНАА (46):** fast and charming. He dodges with a joke and pays with promises. He swears like breathing: «пизда» for bad luck, and «алны юм» for objects. Frightened, he goes short and the curse comes out flat. **After the bite his banter loses its curses**; only the fear-curses stay. He never explains himself.
- **ЦЭЦЭГМАА (~30):** efficient, dry, knows every dodge. Her cruelty is one sentence long and always deserved. She does not swear all day. The one time she does is V25.
- **АЛТАН (3):** one to three words. Repeats what matters, including her father's «новш».
- **ДУЛМАА (65):** practical authority who judges the neighbours easily. Kind with her hands, hard with her mouth. Her swearing is an old woman's «новш» about people, never at family.
- **ХҮРЭЛ (50s):** debt-keeper and feeder. His insults are affection («мангар», «алны юм»), and his «новш» is for men who deserve it.
- **СҮХЭЭ:** warm when drunk and invasive. He helps too much. «Новш минь» is how he says *friend*.
- **ТОГОО (40s):** a moocher who used to sleep in the heating-pipe tunnel. His jokes arrive a beat late, and he is always thirsty. He never swears in front of the people he asks for credit. He knows hunger, so he feeds the boy.
- **БАТАА (60):** retired heating-plant man. Dry, newspaper humour about the state and the heating. He swears like an old man: «золиг».
- **НЯМКА:** shopkeeper. Writes everything down and forgives quietly.
- **ПРОФЕССОР (55):** homeless former teacher. Formal, precise, and darkly funny; his jokes are about poverty and are always true. He uses «гөлөг» as a teacher's word. The kindest man in town and the dirtiest.
- **БИЛГҮҮН (8):** the boy upstairs. Flat, factual, in shock. He never cries on camera.
- **ИРИНА:** clipped, exhausted, exact; one dry sentence about vodka, and she never swears. **ДАША:** almost no words. **ПАША:** an unpaid Russian loader who jokes about the rouble. «Бля» is his comma.
- **Minor townspeople** (kiosk seller, Khand, chenzh woman, customs officer, card player, policeman): each says one ugly true thing in their own rhythm. They are not all sarcastic. Most mean exactly what they say. Market women, the card player, the policeman and the drivers swear freely. Khand, Nyamka, Tungalag (except once, in fright), Irina, Dasha and the children do not.

**Period rule (v5.1):** Naran, late autumn 1998, a few months after the August rouble crash (~21 ₽/$). Tögrög ≈ 840 ₮/$. An average wage is about 34,000₮ a month, a loaf is under 300₮, and a bottle of vodka is a few thousand tögrög. Dollars belong to traders and ченжүүд, not to a railway doctor. Every price in the script follows `research-1998-notes.md`.

# ACT I · THE WAGON

## V01 · 00:00–01:30 — Ночлежка / The rented wagon
**EXT. ULAN-UDE FREIGHT YARD — PRE-DAWN (~05:50)**

▸ Black. A freight coupling bangs. No music.
▸ Frost on a boxcar's chalked number. A gloved hand counts a fold of roubles and a few tögrög notes under a flashlight. The **YARD GUARD** (Russian, 50s) pockets them, then raps his keys twice on the wagon door.

**ХАРУУЛ** *(Russian; routine, impatient)*: Подъём, бля! Выходи, давай. [хадмал: Бос, пизда! Гараад ир.] `EP01-VO-001`

▸ He slides the door a hand's width. Warm stink. He turns his face away.

**ХАРУУЛ**: Слышь, утро уже. Деньги брал до утра, а не до обеда. [хадмал: Хөөе, өглөө боллоо. Мөнгийг чинь өглөө хүртэл гэж авсан, үд хүртэл биш.] `EP01-VO-002`

▸ From inside: wet breathing, and something rhythmic. A cheap Chinese thermos rolls into the gap and stops.

**ХАРУУЛ** *(lower)*: Эй... вы там живые? [хадмал: Хөөе... тэнд амьд байна уу?] `EP01-VO-003`

▸ He shoves the door half a metre. The flashlight finds straw, bundled sleepers, and a crouched back working over a lying shape. We see only the back and the steam rising off it.
▸ A **YOUNG WOMAN** bursts out of the dark past his legs: **pink mohair scarf, green tracksuit jacket, chequered trader's bag**. She clutches her forearm and is gone between the wagons. *(This is DASHA. Hold her scarf in frame for one clear beat.)*
▸ The crouched back stops. It turns. The flashlight catches a flushed, wet face for a few frames, then spins away into the gravel.

**ХАРУУЛ** *(one scream, cut off)*: А-а! Сука!.. [хадмал: А-а! Гичий!..] `EP01-V5-001`

▸ The figure is already past him, crossing the yard at an impossible speed: over a coupling, along a fence, past a lit window.
▸ **INT. DISPATCH OFFICE WINDOW:** **IRINA** (40s, railway medical officer) presses RECORD on a dictaphone and looks at her watch.
▸ Four panels: the watch's second hand. The figure vaulting a coupling. The watch. The figure slowing, slowing, then turning toward a **steam pipe beside her office, twenty metres from her glass**. It stops in the steam and stands.

**ИРИНА** *(Russian; into the dictaphone, close)*: Снова. Примерно три минуты... и опять к теплу. [хадмал: Дахиад. Ойролцоогоор гурван минут... тэгээд дахиад л дулаан руу.] `EP01-VO-004`

**ИРИНА**: Девушка. Укус на предплечье. Ушла. [хадмал: Нэг охин. Шуунаасаа хазуулсан. Явчихлаа.] `EP01-V5-002`

▸ She stops the tape before her voice goes.
▸ By the wagon, the guard is on his knees, his hand bitten and dripping onto the gravel. With that same hand he picks the scattered notes off the ground, one by one.
