# ХАНИАД EP01 v5: what changed and why

**For:** the manager and the creator. **Script:** `EP01_v5_FULL_SCRIPT.md`. **Beats:** `EP01_v5_BEATS.csv`. **Assets:** `EP01_v5_NEW_ASSETS.md`.
**Source baseline:** `EP01_v4.2_FULL_SCRIPT_READTHROUGH_CANDIDATE.md` and the v4.3 recording manifest (197 cues). No existing file was edited.

## 0 · What stayed (the spine)

The core story is unchanged:
- The Ulan-Ude yard and Irina's three-minute sprint observation.
- The satchel she gives Ganaa for the Naran duty doctor.
- The long border drive.
- Dasha, the shared enamel bowl (kindness, not infection) and the birthday meat.
- Hürel's café, the debt banter and «Битгий шаа».
- The attack, and Dasha biting Ganaa's **LEFT wrist**.
- The local fire, with the satchel left in the disabled KAMAZ.
- The livestock truck, the flu radio, the **protected line verbatim** (V18, `EP01-VO-142`) and the silent sheep.
- Ganaa refusing the hospital.
- The party, the lie, «Дараа», the bone crack, the slightly-too-big mittens and the off-key song.
- The guest infant crying, «Цэцгээ... хүүхдүүдийг...», the **inward-opening, lockless** kitchen door, Sükhee **pushing**, and the cut to black on the exhale/inhale.

Of the 197 v4.3 cues, 150 are kept word for word, 11 are edited, 36 are cut, and 108 lines are new (269 spoken lines in total).

**Hard-fact check:**
- Left-wrist bite: V16, V18, V23.
- Inward door with no lock: V05 insert, V24, V25.
- The infected only push or slam doors: wagon, office window, Saraa's door, truck door, kitchen.
- No traffic lights, and no pharmacy scene.
- The bowl is explicitly not the infection route (V10).
- Altan is 3.
- The protected line is verbatim, confirmed by grep.

## 5 · The philosophy thread, beat by beat (shown, never said)

**Fragile · greedy · fake.** No character ever names any of these words.

1. **V01 (greedy):** the guard rents a wagon to the poor and picks up his money with a bitten hand.
2. **V02 (fake):** «Собака.» «Не пиши никуда... у меня семья.» The first "dog".
3. **V03 (greedy/fake):** a child is refused bread on credit, and a dead man's boot is bought for the price of socks. «Тэр нохой чинь хоёр хөлтэй.» The town blames the homeless for the flu and wants them locked up.
4. **V04 (fake):** «Ганаа ирэхээр» is the district's currency, a whole economy of promises. Nobody counts the vanished homeless. «Тэндээс хүн хаашаа ч явдаггүй.»
5. **V05 (fake):** vodka is hidden from guests. Saraa is judged («Өөрөө л тэр»). A neighbour needles Tsetsegmaa with a smile.
6. **V06 (fragile):** an unpaid loader, a rouble that died, a "rabid bomzh" everyone walks around.
7. **V07 (greedy):** Ganaa accepts money for a promise.
8. **V08 (fragile):** the only person who understood dies of a phone call.
9. **V09 (greedy):** the warning money buys a stamp.
10. **V10 (greedy):** traders dump a bleeding girl and keep her fare. Ganaa's foot lifts before he brakes.
11. **V11 (human):** the "monster" pays for her meal with chocolate for a stranger's child.
12. **V12 (fake):** Khand's gossip, then her compliment. A child's literal sentence becomes grammar. A door check becomes a joke. A boy with a key is sent out with buuz.
13. **V14/V15 (greedy):** the border gets paid and the friend doesn't. A girl gets priced like goods.
14. **V16 (no justice):** the kind man dies and the thief lives, betrayed by his partner.
15. **V18 (official lie):** "flu" and «Ямар алных нь томуу вэ...»
16. **V19 (fragile/fake):** the bitten become drunks and criminals, so everyone hides.
17. **V20–V21 (fake):** «Юманд зүсчихсэн.» «Дараа.» Party noise covers the thumps upstairs.
18. **V22 (the first refusal):** Tsetsegmaa pockets a bloody chocolate without a word.
19. **V24–V25 (fragile):** Ganaa's one good act traps a man stealing vodka. Love doesn't save anyone. A bottle rolls to a child's foot.

## 6 · "How did it get out?": decision

Included, but as **how it got out, not where it came from**. The cold open shows the escape mechanism: a guard's paid flophouse wagon, a hidden bite, and sleepers riding freight to Naran four days earlier. It is human greed, with no lab and no lecture. The biological origin stays unexplained, as the bible requires ("Season 1 doesn't explain the entire pandemic"). A science-origin scene would have been the boring version.

## 7 · Runtime and density

- **Runtime: 26:00** including the 10 s end card. That is +1:00 over the 25:00 target and inside the ±2:00 band. The extra minute is the V12 midpoint (2:10). It is the scene that makes the ending land, so I would rather keep it than make the episode two minutes shorter.
- **Spoken words: ~1,550** (v4.2 had ~1,118). That is about 11 minutes of speech in 26, which leaves room for silence and action. The densest scenes are V04 (161 words in 80 s) and V14 (126 in 75 s), the same risk v4.2 flagged for the café. The table read decides.
