### Who used dollars
- From 1991 the ports to China opened and "suitcase trading" grew up. Mongolians filled the gap left by the end of Soviet trade. CONFIRMED: Taylor & Francis, "Collaboration for survival in the age of the market": https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02634937.2018.1501347
- Informal trade from China to Russia through Mongolia ran along the Trans-Mongolian railway. The people in it include **wholesalers (*chanjuud*, ченжүүд)**, **retailers (*naimaachin*, наймаачин)**, moneychangers and cross-border drivers. SOURCED (search excerpt; the page returned 405 when fetched): Open Book Publishers, *Frontier Encounters*, ch. 7: https://books.openbookpublishers.com/10.11647/obp.0026/14_Chapter7.html
- **What follows for the script:** dollars belong to traders, ченжүүд, moneychangers and drivers who cross the border, not to a Russian railway doctor whose own wages are in roubles that just lost 70% of their value. That is why Irina's **$20 was unrealistic**. It is about 16,800 ₮, half a Mongolian monthly wage, or about 420 ₽ after the crash. Her 100 ₽ is a real sacrifice for her and little to him.

## 2 · Period (nostalgia)

### «Халтар царайт» (*Cara sucia*)
- A Venezuelan telenovela from Venevisión, first aired 5 Mar–12 Oct 1992. Characters: **Estrella** (Sonya Smith), the poor newspaper seller; **Miguel Ángel** (Guillermo Dávila), the rich heir; **Santa** (Gigi Zanchetta), his scheming ex. CONFIRMED: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cara_sucia_(TV_series)
- **Mongolian broadcast: MNB (national TV), 1994–1995, 7:00 p.m.** CONFIRMED (Wikipedia broadcasters table, same page). It was dubbed into Mongolian. The voices of Estrella and Miguel Ángel were MNB presenters **Гантигмаа** and **Бямбацогт**: https://news.zindaa.mn/24px . It aired in eight countries, Mongolia among them: https://ikon.mn/opinion/26vq
- It is remembered as "the series that kept people glued to the blue screen" (zindaa). It is on the "90s things Gen Z won't understand" list: https://www.ub.life/p/z-ueiinkhnii-khezee-ch-oilgokhgui-90-eed-onii-14-zuil
- **Script consequence:** EP01 is set in **1998**, three to four years **after** the broadcast, so the show cannot be "on tonight" without an unconfirmed rerun. v5.1 uses it as what it was in 1998: a shared memory everyone still quotes. Khand calls the Ganaa gossip «Яг л "Халтар царайт"». The creator's "every street was empty" memory is true to 1994–95; if the creator remembers a **1998 rerun**, the empty-street joke can go in (see the pass doc, §9).
