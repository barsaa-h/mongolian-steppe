## 1 · Your five notes, and what was done

1. **Curses.** Swearing now goes where a Mongolian in 1998 actually swears: at a broken thing, at bad luck, in fright, between men who love each other, at people "below" you, and in Russian on the Russian side. It is never in the tender scenes (V11, V13, V21–V24). Two rules give it shape:
   - **Ganaa's banter stops swearing after the bite.** In V14 he swears like breathing. From V20 on, only fear-curses are left. His coldness is the symptom.
   - **Tsetsegmaa swears once in the whole episode,** at the very end, so it lands. Her three-year-old says «Новш» before she does, because she learned it from Dad.
2. **Dark humour.** The dog-eating joke is gone. It is replaced with jokes real people make about the state, the heating, vodka, debt, roubles, the militsiya and death (§2). Each joke is placed so it gets a laugh and then hurts a minute later: Hürel's headstone joke comes two minutes before he dies, and the Professor's "double the knot" comes when he knows what he will become.
3. **«Ээж дүүг идэж байна»** and **«Хөхүүлж байна гэдэг юм, миний хүү. Идэж биш.»** are kept word for word. The older guest's setup now says «хөхүүлээч» («тэр гичий хүүхдээ нэг юм хөхүүлээч дээ»), so the crying stops on the word *feed* and Khand's correction echoes it. It is still implied only.
4. **No plot armour, but reasonable.** No new deaths. Each existing death is caused by story logic, and each victim now has a human moment before we lose them (§5).
5. **Unanswered:** v5 is kept as written for both (§9). Dasha's bite stays in the cold open, and the runtime stays 26:00 (the 25:04 cut list in `EP01_v5_CHANGES.md` §7 still works).

## 6 · Money and period changes (scope update)

Full sources are in `research-1998-notes.md`.

**Findings:**
- **Rate.** In 1998 the tögrög was about **840 ₮/$** (period average).
- **Wages and poverty.** The average wage was about **34,500 ₮ a month (≈ $40)**. The official poverty line was **14,674 ₮ per person per month**, and 35.6% of people were poor.
- **Prices.** A loaf cost **290 ₮** and vodka **2,700 ₮** in 1999, so a loaf was about **250–290 ₮** in 1998.
- **Notes.** The **5,000 ₮** note (printed 1994) and the **10,000 ₮** note (1995) were in circulation.
- **The rouble.** It fell from 6.3 to about **21 ₽/$** after August 1998.
- **Dollars.** They belonged to ченжүүд (wholesale suitcase traders), наймаачид, moneychangers and cross-border drivers.

**Why $20 was wrong.** For a Russian railway doctor paid in crashed roubles, $20 is about 420 ₽, or 16,800 ₮, which is half a Mongolian monthly wage. She would not carry a green bill or give it to a stranger.
