# ХАНИАД — THE COUGH

*Series bible v0.2 · 15 episodes × 8–9 minutes · Naran, Mongolia · Autumn 1998*

**Logline.** Autumn 1998. A trucker brings a bite home for his daughter’s birthday. Over twenty days, a doctor, a governor, a schoolgirl with a camcorder, a deserter and a family of horse riders fight their way out of a dying Mongolian town toward the one place the dead can’t follow: the cold.

**Tagline.** *Nobody panics at a cold.*

**The pitch in one breath.** Fast, hungry, classic zombies. One clean rule about meat and energy. A ten-year-old who counts seconds. A lie from the governor’s office. And a clock: the gates of the Cold Station close on Day 21.

## 1. What changed from v0.1

- **Cut:** the non-linear time-cards, the Courier frame, the one-POV-per-chapter mood structure, and the silent “Quiet” infected who never attack.
- **Kept:** Naran 1998, the whole cast, kindness as the danger (the generous get bitten first), the cold as mercy, “nobody is punished,” Anu’s camcorder, Irina’s tapes, Dorj’s eagle dance, the nursery key, Tsogt’s long song.
- **New:** classic fast infected with one simple energy rule, one linear timeline (Day 0 → Day 20), a countdown (gates close Day 21), and an 8:30 episode engine where something changes every 60–90 seconds.

## 2. The rules (clean enough for a ten-year-old)

Four rules. Each one is shown in action before anyone explains it. They also double as your story tools: chase, stealth, decoy, dilemma.

**RULE 1 — THE BITE.** A bite, a deep scratch, or infected blood in an open wound infects you. First comes **the Cough**: fever, sweat, shaking, and a sudden craving for meat. Then you turn. The delay is **the Clock**: neck or face about 1 hour, torso 3–4 hours, hand or foot up to 12 hours. On screen, a thin red line creeps from the bite toward the heart. A cough on its own means nothing (flu, stove smoke). Only a bite counts.

**RULE 2 — THE MEAL.** The infected hunt the living to eat them. **Eaten stays. Bitten returns.** If you die in the attack, you’re food and you stay dead. If you survive the attack with a bite, you turn when the Clock runs out. Why people survive bites: the infected burn out mid-attack (Rule 3), or something fresher runs past.

**RULE 3 — THE BURN.** The infected run like furnaces. They hunt by sound and the smell of blood first, sight second (a gunshot is a dinner bell). When they lock onto prey they sprint at full speed for **about 180 seconds**. Then they stagger. Then they stop. **Meat resets the bar:** one sheep, one dog, one person, and they get another full Burn.

**RULE 4 — THE STANDING AND THE COLD.** At zero energy they stop and stand, drifting toward heat: boilers, south walls, hot springs. The Standing wake to noise, touch, or blood, but wake low on energy (one short Burn unless they feed). Below about −20°C they freeze solid. Thaw wakes them. *Cold is mercy; the thaw is the clock for Season 2.*

**Colour grammar (readable with no dialogue):**

- **RED:** fed, flushed, steaming, blood on the mouth. Fast. Run.
- **GREY:** sunken, trembling, slow. Dangerous only up close.
- **WHITE:** still, frosted, standing. Asleep. Do not make a sound.

**Story tools the rules give you**

- *Chase:* Sarnai counts the Burn out loud. 180 seconds is the length of every chase.
- *Stealth:* the Standing. Every step matters; every cough is a gun.
- *Decoy:* throw meat (a sheep, a horse) and the Burn restarts somewhere else.
- *Dilemma:* a hidden bite and a twelve-hour clock. Who is hiding one?
- *Siege:* heat. The dead gather wherever it’s warm, which is exactly where the living want to shelter.

## 3. The 8:30 episode engine

Every episode is the same machine with a different engine inside. Script length is about one page per minute: 8–9 pages.

| Time | Beat | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | **HOOK** | Start mid-danger. No setup. |
| 0:45–1:45 | **SETUP** | Who, what they want, a visible clock. |
| 1:45–3:15 | **ESCALATION 1** | First complication, first rule shown. |
| 3:15–4:30 | **SET PIECE A** | Loud, big, physical. |
| 4:30–5:00 | **MIDPOINT** | A reversal or reveal that changes the goal. |
| 5:00–6:30 | **ESCALATION 2** | Worse. A hard choice. |
| 6:30–7:45 | **SET PIECE B** | Bigger and a different flavour from A. |
| 7:45–8:30 | **COST + CLIFFHANGER** | Someone or something is lost. The last 10 seconds ask a question. |

**Rules of the room**

1. Something changes every 60–90 seconds: a new threat, a reveal, a reversal, or a choice.
2. No scene is quiet for more than 40 seconds unless a clock is visibly ticking.
3. Every episode teaches or twists exactly one rule, by action.
4. Every episode has a **Cost**: a death, a betrayal, or a loss that sticks.
5. Alternate loud and silent set pieces inside the episode.
6. Every episode has its own look or genre so the season never feels repetitive.
7. The last image is a question, not an answer.

## 5. Cast

- **SARNAI (10)**, child jockey. Counts everything. Wants her mother. Her counting becomes the group’s weapon. *Survives.*
- **TSOGT (40)**, herder, her father. Few words, huge hands. *Dies singing (Ep 15).*
- **DORJ (71)**, retired wrestler, Tsogt’s father. *The eagle dance (Ep 6).*
- **NARANTUYA (27)**, archer, Tsogt’s sister. Her bow is the silent weapon. *Holds the exit (Ep 15).*
- **BOLD (19)**, cousin who talks too much. *Takes the noise (Ep 15).*
- **DR. OYUN (38)**, obstetrician. Counts everything, sleeps three hours. She locks the door (Ep 2). *Survives, guilty.*
- **ERDENE (19)**, conscript. Obeys because he never learned anything else. *Learns to choose. Survives.*
- **ANUDARI “ANU” (16)**, Hi8 camcorder. Films because it’s easier than feeling. *Bitten (Ep 12). Walks back into the valley.*
- **GOVERNOR LKHAGVASUREN “LKHAGVA” (58)**, soft-spoken, reasonable, the lie. *Lives. Chairs the Council.*
- **LT. BAYAR (35)**, sells passage at the cordon. *Lives.*
- **GANAA (46)** and **DASHA (24)**, trucker and hitchhiker. *Ep 1 only.*
- **IRINA KOVALEVA (54)**, epidemiologist. A voice on tapes from Ep 1 on. *Never seen after Ep 1.*
- **NARANTSETSEG**, Tsogt’s wife. **NARAN**, their baby, born Ep 2. **MUNKH**, the boy voted out (Ep 4).

Eight leads go into the valley. Four come out: Sarnai, baby Naran, Oyun, Erdene. (Plus a governor, because nobody is punished.)

## 6. Episode beat sheets

### ACT I — THE FALL

### EP 1 · SHARE THE BOWL

**Day 0 · Ganaa · road horror → dread · teaches: the Bite and the Clock**

- **0:00 Hook —** Ulan-Ude freight yard, night. A guard slides open a boxcar. Something flushed and steaming covers forty metres in four seconds and takes his throat. From a window, Irina mutters into her dictaphone: “Subject runs three minutes. Then he falls asleep standing.”
- **0:45 Setup —** Irina shoves a satchel of tapes at Ganaa: “Ministry of Health. Today.” He’s loud, generous, singing, with eleven hours to reach his daughter Altan’s third birthday.
- **1:45 Escalation —** Border road. Hitchhiker Dasha, bandaged forearm, coughing. He shares his tea bowl. She eats his whole cooler of mutton with her bare hands. He laughs. Then he stops laughing.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** Truck-stop chase. Dasha turns mid-dumpling order and sprints beside the KAMAZ at 45 km/h. Ganaa rams a fuel pump; fireball. Dasha is dead. So is the cook she was eating, and he stays dead.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Ganaa’s hand: a clean bite. A thin red line starts at his wrist. “It’s a scratch. Birthday first, hospital after.”
- **5:00 Escalation —** HOUR 5: the line reaches his elbow. Sweat, shakes, and a flock of sheep he cannot stop staring at.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** The party: forty guests, a roast lamb, a cake. He devours three plates and everyone cheers. The line crosses his collar. His wife: “You’re burning.” He keeps singing.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** Altan climbs into his lap, candles lit. Mid-song Ganaa stops singing. In the next room, a baby cries. His head turns. Black.

**Cost:** Ganaa, Dasha, the party, and a satchel nobody opened.

### EP 2 · THE NURSERY

**Day 0 night → Day 1 dawn · Dr. Oyun · hospital thriller · teaches: Eaten stays, bitten returns**

- **0:00 Hook —** 2 a.m. Oyun counts nine newborn cries behind the nursery glass. The ER doors burst open: bleeding birthday guests, a wife screaming, “He bit our neighbour!”
- **0:45 Setup —** Narantsetseg, Tsogt’s wife, is in labour in Room 4. Twelve patients, one phone, and the Governor’s voice on it: “Seasonal flu. Keep calm.”
- **1:45 Escalation —** Oyun triages by bite. Two torn-open victims stay dead. A man bitten an hour ago convulses, rises, and kills a doctor. She says the rule out loud: “Eaten stays. Bitten returns.”
- **3:15 Set piece A —** Corridor dash with the night nurse, Bolor, dragging mothers to the ward. Fed infected sprint. One burns out and stands facing the boiler pipe. Oyun learns that they stop.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Narantsetseg is bitten shielding a neighbour’s baby. Three hours on her Clock. “Deliver her first.”
- **5:00 Escalation —** C-section by flashlight while the door is hammered. Oyun orders everyone to show their arms and legs. Bolor lies about her thigh.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** Baby Naran is born. Narantsetseg: “Tell Tsogt her name is Naran.” Bolor turns at the nursery glass. Fourteen people in the ward, three hiding bites, forty Hungry in the corridor. The Governor’s order arrives by phone: seal the ward. The lock is on the outside. Oyun turns the key. We never see inside. We hear.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** Out through the laundry hatch with Naran in her coat. Dawn in the car park: hundreds of the Standing facing the boiler house. A car radio: “Weather today: clear and sunny.”

**Cost:** the ward behind the door. Oyun’s guilt starts here.

### EP 3 · SEASONAL INFLUENZA

**Day 1 · Governor Lkhagva · political thriller, black comedy · teaches: noise is a dinner bell**

- **0:00 Hook —** Live radio, a voice like warm tea: “…seasonal influenza. Stay calm, stay home.” Camera pulls back: he’s in a locked bathroom, his bodyguard bleeding in the tub. He finishes the broadcast, then whispers, “Quiet, please.”
- **0:45 Setup —** The Minister on the phone: “Don’t be a hero. Report flu.” His logic: say plague, the border closes, the fuel stops, the town starves anyway. His clock: one helicopter, six seats, a napkin with names.
- **2:00 Escalation —** Oyun calls from the hospital: “There are children here.” He orders the ward sealed and stays on the line until it goes dead.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** The privatization dinner in the Governor’s hall: caviar, vodka, a bleeding guest. The bodyguard rises in the corridor. A suits-versus-the-Hungry chase through the banquet, the napkin soaked in wine.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Ger-district families gather in the square, shouting for buses. He steps onto the balcony: “Buses at dawn.” There are no buses. Their shouting pulls the Hungry in from three streets.
- **5:00 Escalation —** He crosses names off the napkin: a doctor out, a cousin in.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** The helipad. The crowd followed him. The pilot refuses to wait. The Governor fires his pistol in the air to clear the pad. The sound sets off the massacre. The helicopter lifts with five of six seats.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** From above: Naran’s heating-plant chimneys glowing, thousands of the Standing ringed round them like a clock face. His daughter-in-law: “Why are they all standing like that?” “They’re waiting for the buses.”

**Cost:** the ger district. Also his soul, but he doesn’t notice.

### EP 4 · BATTERY: 38 MINUTES

**Day 2 · Anu · found footage (4:3 Hi8, timecode, battery icon) · teaches: the Standing wake to noise**

- **0:00 Hook —** Battery 38:00. Anu films a friend’s fake cough as a joke. The PE teacher stumbles in with a bleeding hand. Three seconds later the first classmate dies on tape.
- **0:45 Setup —** School No. 2 becomes a shelter: 200 kids, 12 teachers, food for four days. The headmistress bolts the gates against the ger-district kids outside. A radio in the staff room: “seasonal flu.”
- **2:00 Escalation —** The headmistress: “Anyone who coughs goes to the gym storage.” Half the school has a cough from stove smoke. Anu knows only bites matter. She says nothing. She keeps filming.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** The vote. Two hundred hands decide who stays. Munkh, who has stove smoke in his lungs and nothing else, is voted out. Anu films it. His knocking at the doors becomes the soundtrack.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Battery 21:00. The PE teacher is locked in the equipment room, standing very still facing the window. A girl in the third row has a hidden bite on her calf.
- **5:00 Escalation —** Night in the gym. Two hundred teenagers must stay silent. Someone coughs. The teacher wakes and the Burn begins.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** The chase through the dark school. Anu alone in the boiler room, whispering a confession to her lens: “I voted yes.”
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** Battery 00:08. She crawls out through the coal chute into the street. Black. Four seconds of audio: the knocking has stopped.

**Cost:** Munkh. And Anu’s promise to herself.

### EP 5 · THE CORDON

**Day 2–3 · Erdene · crowd standoff, fog · teaches: Fed versus Grey (the energy is visible)**

- **0:00 Hook —** Fog. A volley. Shapes sprinting out of the white. Erdene, 19, a rifle too big for him, fires at a person for the first time. SUPER: SIX HOURS EARLIER.
- **0:45 Setup —** The cordon on the only road out is a bazaar. Lt. Bayar sells passage by the sack of flour. The Governor’s cousin’s jeep is waved through. A mother with a coughing child (stove smoke again) is turned back.
- **2:00 Escalation —** Six hundred people, stoves on their backs. A man in the line hides a red line under his sleeve. Erdene sees it. Bayar: “Shoot anyone who coughs.” Erdene doesn’t.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** A father offers a gold ring. Bayar takes it and still waves the family back. The father charges. A shot. The crowd screams.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** In the field behind them, unseen, the Standing wake. Three hundred heads turn together.
- **5:00 Escalation —** The stampede. RED ones sprint, GREY ones stagger. Bayar throws the sacks of flour in his jeep and drives off through the crowd.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** Erdene holds the sandbags with a woman, three kids, and twelve bullets, counting. At the three-minute mark the sprinters slow, stagger, and stand. He understands. They stop.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** He drops the rifle and walks east. On the ridge, a woman aims a bow at his chest: “Don’t move.” Behind him, a new Burn starts.

**Cost:** the town’s last way out. Bayar escapes with the flour.

### ACT II — THE RIDE

### EP 6 · FELT

**Day 3–4 · Tsogt · horse western → horror · teaches: the Burn is 180 seconds, and Sarnai can count it**

- **0:00 Hook —** Narantuya’s arrow stops an inch from Erdene’s eye. A RED runner hits the ridge behind him. Her second arrow drops it. Erdene: “Take me with you.” Tsogt: “Can you ride?” “No.”
- **0:45 Setup —** Why the riders are riding toward Naran: a radio reading names from the hospital. Narantsetseg’s name isn’t among them. Hooves wrapped in felt. Dorj: “A wrestler doesn’t run.”
- **2:00 Escalation —** Night ride. A fed runner chases Sarnai, the lightest rider, on her racing mare. She counts aloud: “…one-seventy-eight, one-seventy-nine, one-eighty.” It falls. She has just invented the rule the others will live by.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** The Gorge. A hundred Hungry pour in behind them. They can’t outrun fresh ones in a narrow pass. Dorj dismounts, folds his arms, and does the wrestler’s eagle dance in the mouth of the gorge, slow motion, a hero’s stand.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Dorj throws a runner off the cliff, spins, throws another. He’s counting too. He holds for two minutes fifty-five seconds.
- **5:00 Escalation —** The family rides on. Tsogt doesn’t look back. Sarnai does, counting.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** Dawn at Naran’s heating plant: thousands of the Standing, steaming in the cold. The riders weave between them on felted hooves. Sarnai recognises her old teacher. She holds her breath.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** In Bold’s pocket the radio crackles into life. A song, loud. A thousand heads begin to turn.

**Cost:** Dorj.

### EP 7 · THE CAR PARK

**Day 4 · Tsogt and Oyun · rescue thriller, heartbreak · teaches: stealth through the Standing**

- **0:00 Hook —** Oyun in the boiler house, Naran in her coat, a scalpel at the door. Felted hooves. The door opens: Tsogt. She puts a baby in his arms. He didn’t know he had a second daughter.
- **0:45 Setup —** “Where is Narantsetseg?” Oyun can’t answer. Radio-song crisis: Bold flips it off. Nobody breathes.
- **1:45 Escalation —** Tsogt steps out into the car park: eight hundred Standing facing the boiler heat. “Don’t,” Oyun says. He walks anyway.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** Stealth. The only way to the horses runs through the field of the Standing. Bold sneezes. One figure stirs and takes three steps. Narantuya’s bow, silent. The arrow flies.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Tsogt finds his wife in the third row, face to the sunrise. Narantuya raises the bow. Tsogt lowers it with his hand. He stands beside Narantsetseg for the whole sunrise. Nobody moves.
- **5:15 Escalation —** “We’ll come back at the thaw,” he says. Oyun and Erdene help the family mount. Sarnai counts the faces: “Seven riders and a baby.”
- **6:30 Set piece B —** An engine. Lt. Bayar’s jeep roars into the car park, horn blaring, to cut through the crowd. Eight hundred wake. The riders run. Bayar floors it through the Standing, laughing, and gets out. He is not punished.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** Dawn. They’re out. In Erdene’s pack, a radio voice: “Cold Station calling. The gates close on Day 21. Come north.” Overhead, a helicopter passes, heading the same way.

**Cost:** Narantsetseg, and Tsogt’s promise.

### EP 8 · THE GREEN OCEAN

**Day 5–6 · Sarnai · open-steppe chase, truck-stop siege · teaches: meat resets the Burn (the decoy)**

- **0:00 Hook —** A flat golden sea of grass. Twenty RED runners chase seven riders at full gallop. Sarnai counts: “…ninety, ninety-one…” The horses are tiring before the runners are.
- **0:45 Setup —** Why so many? A slaughtered flock lies behind them. They fed. They reset. Sarnai: “Meat gives them more.”
- **2:00 Escalation —** Food is running out. Bold hunts a sheep. The blood on the wind calls more.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** The Decoy Run. Narantuya slings a sheep carcass off the pack horse. The runners stop to feed. Gap: four hundred metres. The first clever trick of the season.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** A truck stop with its lights on: Ganaa’s KAMAZ, empty, door open, the satchel on the seat. Irina’s tape: “They stop at about three minutes. They hate the cold.” A radio: “Gates close Day 21. Fifteen days left.”
- **5:00 Escalation —** Under the counter, a sixteen-year-old girl with a dead camcorder and a bag of tapes: Anu. “Don’t film me. Wait. Can I film you?”
- **6:30 Set piece B —** Forty runners off the highway. The group barricades in the truck bay. Erdene opens the fuel line. Narantuya lights an arrow. The fireball takes the pumps, the forecourt, and half the runners.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** A helicopter limps out of the smoke on fumes and lands. The Governor steps out with a pistol and a smile: “Friends.”

**Cost:** the truck stop, and every horse’s last easy day.

### EP 9 · TWO SEATS

**Day 7–9 · Lkhagva and Oyun · standoff, betrayal · teaches: paranoia (who is hiding a bite?)**

- **0:00 Hook —** Dawn. Erdene’s rifle is an inch from the Governor’s forehead. “Say it again.” SUPER: TWO HOURS EARLIER.
- **0:45 Setup —** The Governor’s backstory in one line: Ulaanbaatar airfield was overrun; the pilot barely got out. He offers tea, charm, and “four seats” on a helicopter with no fuel.
- **2:00 Escalation —** His bodyguard Batu is hiding a bite. Oyun sees it. The Governor: “Shoot him now and save the horses.” Tsogt: “He is a man.”
- **3:15 Set piece A —** Irina’s second tape: “Close the border now. It is Day minus four.” The Governor’s log shows he knew weeks ago. The room goes quiet.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Batu turns in the helicopter cabin, a tight Burn in a metal box. Cousin Dashnyam dies. Eaten, he stays.
- **5:00 Escalation —** Bayar’s deserters arrive in a truck: three armed men who want the horses and the Governor.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** A three-way standoff. Bayar fires in the air to cow them. The Hungry come off the highway. Everyone has to work together: the Governor pushes Batu’s body toward the runners as a decoy and keeps his pistol.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** When the sun comes back, the Governor unfolds a map. The only road to the Cold Station runs through the Warm Valley. Tsogt: “How many?” “Everyone who waited for the buses.”

**Cost:** the party’s trust in the Governor. He keeps his chair.

### EP 10 · DZUD

**Day 10–14 · Sarnai · survival in the snow · teaches: the Cold**

- **0:00 Hook —** Dawn, −27°C. Sarnai rides along a ridge of frozen figures, glittering like glass. She counts them: “Four hundred and ten.” The first time the cold looks like mercy.
- **0:45 Setup —** The dzud: a killer winter. Horses are dying. Food for four days. Tsogt: “We eat a horse.” Sarnai’s racing mare is on the list.
- **2:00 Escalation —** Bayar’s hungry men follow the tracks through a white-out and arrive at the winter camp. Nomad law: you feed whoever comes to the door, even your enemies.
- **3:15 Set piece A —** Tea, then a night raid. A fight in total silence under a frozen ridge, because one shout would wake the hills. Tsogt kills a man with his hands.
- **4:30 Midpoint —** Bayar steals Sarnai’s mare and rides for the lake.
- **5:00 Escalation —** Sarnai alone, on a pony, in a blizzard, following hoofprints.
- **6:30 Set piece B —** The frozen lake with figures standing in the ice. Bayar’s horse breaks through. Sarnai gets her mare back, then sees a line of RED runners racing across the lake toward Tsogt. She cuts the mare loose as a decoy: “Go.” She cries without a sound.
- **7:45 Cliffhanger —** Day 14. The north pass. Below, steam rising from a valley in −25°C. The Standing here don’t freeze. Ten thousand of them, in the one place that’s warm.

**Cost:** the mare, and the last of Sarnai’s childhood.
