# Brief CS01: Fix the stylized cast builder (hands, eyes, mouth, hair, rig) for the 12 EP01 main characters

**Worker:** Codex. **Priority:** #1. EP01 must be finished in one week, using this cast.

## Why
The user switched EP01 from the realistic MPFB cast to the **stylized low-poly legacy cast** (`assets/cast/*.glb`). It is the cast seen in the HOUR-5 prototype `output/film/khaniad-hour-five.mp4`. Every character is generated by **`scripts/build-cast.py`** from two Vinrax PSX bases (male casual, female secretary), driven by `assets/cast/roster.json`.

The user's own verdict on Ganaa in HOUR 5:
1. **Hands don't look like hands.** The bases have paddle hands; the male rig has one finger bone, and the female rig has only a thumb. Hands must be visible and believable in close shots.
2. **The eyes pop out.** `face_details()` puts white ellipsoid eyes *on top of* the face (`eye-white-*`, `iris-*`, `eyelid-*`). In close-ups they read as bulging white balls.
3. **The hair looks like a hat.** `hairstyle()` puts a uniform hemisphere shell (`*-scalp`) over the head, plus blob locks.
4. **There are two mouths.** The Vinrax atlas has a painted mouth, and `face_details()` adds `mouth-opening` plus `upper-lip`/`lower-lip` meshes on top.

**Fix them in the builder, never by hand-editing a GLB**, so all 52 characters benefit.

## Target look
Silent Hill 2 / PS2-era stylized realism: **low-poly but human, and never cartoon or bug-eyed.** These characters will be rendered in three.js with dark motivated lighting, fog and grain (see `episodes/EP01/threejs/src/look.js`). Keep the budget at **≤ 6k triangles per adult**, textures ≤ 512 px.

## Tasks
### 1. One shared rig contract for male and female bases (the animation code depends on these exact names)
```
pelvis spine chest neck head jaw eye.L eye.R
shoulder_left upper_arm_left forearm_left hand_left      (and _right)
thumb.01.L thumb.02.L thumb.03.L   index.01.L index.02.L index.03.L
middle.01.L middle.02.L middle.03.L ring.01.L ring.02.L ring.03.L
pinky.01.L pinky.02.L pinky.03.L                           (and .R)
thigh_left shin.L foot_left toe.L                          (and _right / .R)
```
- Every bone rolls so that **local +X bends the finger toward the palm (curl)** and **local +Z spreads it**. Write the axis convention in the report.
- `jaw` opens the mouth. `eye.L`/`eye.R` sit at the eyeball centres, so rotating them turns the irises.
- Children and infants keep the same names. The swaddled infant may stay as it is.
- Rename or replace the old `f_middle.01`, `thumb.L`, `*_end` bones. Rebuild Ganaa's `hour-five` clip on the new rig.

### 2. Real hands
- Replace the paddle hands with a low-poly **five-finger hand**: palm + 3 segments per finger, about 300–500 tris per hand. The proportions must be human (palm ≈ finger length; the thumb comes off the side of the palm, not the end).
- Skin it smoothly to the finger bones. The knuckles must bend without collapsing.
- Match the skin tone through the atlas or a skin material. Give the hand subtle knuckle and nail shading in the texture.
- **Rest pose: relaxed natural curl** (fingers slightly bent, thumb relaxed), never flat. Adults, children and gloves/mittens all need to work. The mitten is a separate prop.

### 3. Eyes in sockets, not on the face
- Sink the eyes into the head: carve or deepen the socket in BODY, and give the eyeball a ~1 cm radius sphere centred behind the surface.
- Use a **muted off-white sclera** (`#b9ab98`-ish, matte, roughness ≥ 0.6) and a smaller dark iris with a pupil. The iris must be no more than 45% of the visible eye width.
- **Upper and lower lids** overlap the eyeball so only an almond shape shows. Keep the `blink` shape working (the lids close fully).
- Remove the floating `eye-white-*` / `iris-*` / `eyelid-*` ellipsoids. Keep `gaze` working either through the `eye.L/R` bones or as a shape key.

### 4. One mouth
- Either paint out the atlas mouth and keep one modelled mouth, or keep the atlas mouth and delete the mesh mouth. **Choose whichever looks better in a close-up at 1080p,** and say which you chose.
- `speak` opens it through the `jaw` bone and/or a shape key, showing a dark interior and a hint of teeth. There must be no gap and no second outline.
- Keep the morph names `speak blink fear hunger resolve gaze`, and add `smile` and `frown`.

### 5. Hair that reads as hair
- Replace the hemisphere shell with **hair shaped to the skull**: a hairline with a widow's peak/temples, volume on top, sideburns, and a natural nape.
- Use low-poly hair cards or sculpted shells with a hair-strand texture (dark strands, not flat colour).
- Styles needed: short (Ganaa: short black, slightly unkempt), receding, bob, long, bun, braid, cap. **A real cap is only for roles whose roster says cap.**
- Nothing may float above the scalp or show a gap at the hairline.

### 6. Idle pose and clips
- `idle` must be a **relaxed standing pose**: arms down at the sides (about 10–15° from the body), slight elbow bend, weight on one leg, shoulders down. No A-pose.
- Keep `walk`, `wave` and `cough`. Add `sit` (on a 0.45 m seat) and `talk_idle` (subtle weight shift + one hand gesture).
