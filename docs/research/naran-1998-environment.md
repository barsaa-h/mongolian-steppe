# Naran environment references — late November 1998

Research date: 2026-10-28. For **KHANIAD**, against `artifacts/character-master/sources/world/NARAN_WORLD_BIBLE_v1.0.md`. Naran is fictional; these sources support material culture and regional plausibility, not its proposed population, coordinates or road distances. No reference assets were downloaded or licensed.

## Build direction

Use three overlapping layers: a modest planned apartment/institution core; a newer layer of small trade around existing routes; and irregular fenced household plots with mixed gers, houses and sheds. Give industry and agriculture practical access, storage and maintenance space. Show an inhabited town with uneven upkeep, rather than treating economic transition as universal abandonment.

Keep the Bible's 3–5-floor core, compact school/hospital, heating yard and rail/grain margin as **art-direction proposals**. No source below validates their exact dimensions. Make streets and entrances usable first; weathering and clutter should follow actual water, fuel, foot and vehicle movements.

## Concrete scene kit — artistic proposal

These choices synthesize the evidence; they are not claims about a photographed town.

- **Core:** a few 3–5-floor blocks with distinct entrances/courtyards, one low school, one hospital group and a modest office frontage. Differentiate panel rhythm, plaster repairs, stair windows and roof silhouettes before adding dirt.
- **Commerce:** several small service-window kiosks, a converted storefront and stalls/counters at the market. Use restrained, readable Mongolian signage prepared for language review. Keep exact brands and banknote/price close-ups uncommitted until period references are checked.
- **Khashaas:** skew boundaries and vary lot depth while preserving connected lanes. Mix gers, compact houses, lean-tos and storage; let fence repairs and gates vary by household. Place useful objects: chopped wood, coal, ash container, sled/handcart and lidded water vessels. Do not distribute everything at random.
- **Work edge:** a grain shed/mill silhouette, coal stockpile, unloading access, workshop and one or two parked freight wagons. Roofs and yards can be underused without being collapsed. Keep heavy-truck access distinct from household footpaths.
- **Weather/light:** low winter sun and long shadows; dry frozen wheel ruts; dark tread-worn paths; localized ice at water handling; snow accumulating in lee pockets. Warm windows and stove/plant emissions indicate specific active heat sources. Their exact quantities and colors are look-development decisions.

## Historical traps to avoid

1. **Present-day Ulaanbaatar as a template.** Do not inherit modern density, towers, cladding, uniform replacement windows, advertising, road furniture or fleet composition from a recent photo. The later sources above have explicitly limited uses.
2. **A settlement made entirely of gers.** Fenced plots can contain several kinds of buildings. Their service arrangements matter as much as their silhouette (source 6).
3. **Universal ruin.** Period farming and commerce sources show functioning, adapting systems. Some repairs, vacancies and underuse communicate transition better than making every facade derelict (sources 1–2).
4. **Deep snow as a climate fact.** The measured climate baseline supports cold and low precipitation, not guaranteed deep fresh snow or a precisely timed storm (source 7).
5. **Modernization assigned too early.** A project beginning in the 1990s and ending in 2000 does not prove all its components existed by the story date (source 4).
6. **Modern visual language.** Keep the user's excluded LEDs, modern fleets, QR signs, smartphone props and contemporary shop branding out of the kit. Incandescent/fluorescent practicals, analog controls and restrained painted signs are production choices pending individual prop checks.
7. **False technical precision.** No actual 1998 Naran survey exists. Exact apartment series, lamp fixtures, UAZ/KAMAZ variants, locomotives, historical signage/packaging and daily November weather remain unverified. Do not bake those guesses into hero close-ups as researched facts.
