# NARAN City v1.1 — Review checklist for the art director

Based on the supplied screenshots. This is not a new story bible; it is an inspection and approval checklist for the existing Blender scene.

## Most obvious issues in current screenshots

| Image | Issue visible now | Acceptance evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| `town-overview.png`, `town-plan.png` | Flat/black missing landscape, isolated roads and buildings, repeating neighborhoods | Real continuous terrain, recognizable districts, natural edge, 20 story anchors |
| `residential-quarter.png`, `episode1-apartments.png` | Repeated identical apartment slabs, overly straight access roads | 4+ massing silhouettes, varied weathering/repair, realistic courtyards and services |
| `ganaa-entrance.png` | Reasonable basic entrance, too plain and clean | Specific domestic signs: entrance wear, doorbell/mailboxes, bicycles/vehicles, household evidence; preserve blocking |
| `ger-district.png` | Rectangular clone khashaas with cloned ger/house | 8+ plot layouts, mixed homes and gers, irregular paths, fencing with plausible maintenance differences |
| `market-street.png`, `civic-center.png` | Too little visible shop/market economy, broad empty ground | Kiosks, grocery, pharmacy, bread counter, canteen, street market, bus/taxi waiting point and legible pedestrian paths |
| `north-arrival.png` | Industrial strips with huge void and low function | Real siding/loading/access, grain and machine yard, road connection, distant farm and hill context |
| `town-plan-readable.png` | Schematic streets dominate and built fabric is sparse | Connected urban zones and documented land-use hierarchy; preserve map reference IDs |

## Approval order

- [ ] Backup created and source scene not overwritten
- [ ] Major story-critical site positions and route connectivity unchanged, or alterations marked for approval
- [ ] Terrain/horizon and natural drainage fixed, including camera-level tests
- [ ] Main/secondary/ger-service road hierarchy established; no traffic lights
- [ ] Functional local economy visible in center, apartment zones and ger outskirts
- [ ] Apartment variants no longer repeat identically across neighboring blocks
- [ ] Mixed, credible khashaa variations with real foot/gate access
- [ ] Worn road surfaces respond to traffic, grade and water rather than uniform noise textures
- [ ] Correct late November 1998 pre-outbreak life cues and period props
- [ ] Believable farms and machinery outside built-up residential center
- [ ] Fixed-camera before/after comparison, plus at least four ground-eye-level renders
- [ ] Exact scene version, updated map, changelog and open questions delivered
