# Director's Book — Hürel's Café / EP01 / Vertical Slice v1.0

## Objective

Deliver an ordinary Mongolian roadside café at a believable, lived-in moment. Don't cue the audience to feel fear from the first frame. The change in atmosphere must arrive from **Dasha's chair moving, Hürel's interrupted concern, and Ganaa's delay before acting**. The jokes are not punchlines; the men have known one another for years.

Source: *EP01 production-directed screenplay v2.1*, café scene at `3:15`, cues E01-D023–D033. Must stay compatible with the *10-episode v3.0 story bible*; after this sequence, Ganaa must escape through the main entrance, receive the wrist bite at the truck door, disable the diesel KAMAZ at the separate petrol dispenser, and leave the satchel behind for EP08.

## Actor directions

**Ganaa** — casual, unashamed affection for Hürel; makes fun of debt rather than apologizing. He glances at Dasha because of Hürel's joke, not because of suspicion. When she attacks he takes one shocked beat, then acts too late. Keep his shout rough and personal, not a generic zombie scream.

**Hürel** — quick, familiar ribbing and a natural small smile. First look at Dasha comes while he's still working. At the chair scrape the face should register mild concern, not foreshadowing. D032 starts mid-motion; the sound is abruptly cut by contact.

**Dasha** — not a performing monster during banter. She is tired, sweating, trying to remain seated; the stove/food draws her involuntarily. No snarling or twitching loop until the actual attack. Her rapid movement at 64–67s is a *timing proxy*, subject to locomotion physics and room-blocking review.

**Truck drivers** — keep one conversation going out of focus; a driver laughs once at Hürel's teasing. They are not a canned sitcom audience. One driver is later targeted; their exits must be mapped in the next action sequence.

## Sound layering

- Pre-attack: KAMAZ engine dying outside; kettle lid, pot hiss, card slap, muted conversation and wind working a loose sign. Near-mic dry dialogue against room tone.
- Pivot: a wooden chair dragging across imperfect concrete flooring. A short breath and one contact impact. No jump-scare musical cymbal.
- Attack: tray ringing, shoe skid, abrupt speech interruption. Duck room bed for shock but never mute physical cause/effect completely.
- No copyrighted music is supplied. No sound files or voice recordings were manufactured as "approved" audio assets.

## Look test (not yet rendered)

Warm dirty tungsten inside; desaturated late-autumn overcast outside. Uneven worn plaster, patched tabletop, coal/metal stove, enamel cup, folded newspapers, grease-stained work surfaces. No generic American diner neon. Low-poly topology is allowed, but silhouettes, eye acting and contacts must remain credible. Test **real user character materials** under both interior and window key lighting. The blockout colors are technical tags, not the intended production palette.

## First review questions

- Is 14 × 10 m a believable roadside café for this amount of traffic and seating? Test actual character collision and camera framing.
- Can the camera show Hürel, Dasha, Ganaa and the south door in distinct contiguous directions?
- Can Dasha get from heater to Hürel without intersecting the counter or seating?
- Does Hürel's reaction have enough normal, compassionate hesitation for the horror to hurt?
- Can the physical cause of the accident remain understandable after the later truck collision is blocked?

Final dialogue recordings, sound design mix, gore assets, rigged acting, camera lens approval, photoreal textures, final render or verified `.blend` runtime. These are deliberately separate production tasks, not omitted silently.
