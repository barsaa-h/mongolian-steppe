# Continuity and Physical-Logic Checklist — EP01 Café vs EP08 Return

**Source:** EP01 v2.1 screenplay and v3 Season Story Bible / Naran World Bible v1.0. New measurements marked **[PROPOSED]**.

## Must preserve from canon

- Hürel runs a roadside café by the Upper Junction, about 29 km road distance from Naran in the regional plan (not a GPS location).
- He and Ganaa know each other; the unpaid buuz ledger is an emotional callback in EP08.
- Dasha is already infected, coughs and eats voraciously. **Sharing tea is not the mechanism that infects Ganaa** under the current disease rules.
- Dasha attacks Hürel. Ganaa escapes, but is bitten later at the KAMAZ's driver's-side door, *after the selected vertical slice ends*.
- In the later action, Ganaa's diesel truck side-swipes a **petrol** dispenser and becomes disabled. A damaged truck is still at the site for EP08.
- Irina's satchel survives behind the KAMAZ seat. It must be neither visibly burned nor teleported into the café.
- The café entrance burns; part of the interior and rear/side storage remain accessible later. If the location is made completely gutted, EP08 needs to be rewritten.
- In EP08, surviving characters travel to this junction/café for concrete route/resource reasons, not by random coincidence.

## Episode integration red flags

1. At 84 seconds this test cuts into action; it **does not** prove the bite, truck acceleration, crash, fuel ignition or safe escape.
2. Dasha must not run through a solid counter/table; final animation needs actual rig collisions.
3. Red infection line only appears on Ganaa **after he is bitten**. No line visible in this vertical slice.
4. Forecourt pump should have protective bollards; the actual crash must show why a hose breaks despite those barriers.
5. The fire cannot instantly erase everything while sparing a prop by fiat. Place satchel inside cab shielded from flames and later inspect smoke/heat damage.
6. Keep EP08 return camera angles anchored to the same door, heater, ledger and vehicle position. Ruin state is separate from new geometry.
