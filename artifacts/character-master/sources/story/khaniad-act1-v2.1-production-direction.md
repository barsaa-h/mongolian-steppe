# ХАНИАД — THE COUGH

## ACT I · THE FALL · Episodes 1–5

**v2.1 — Actor-performance & sound direction pass · 28 October 2026 · Based on v2.0**

> **What this is.** A new *working screenplay*, not a silent correction of Series Bible v0.2. The original 1998 Naran setting, characters, tragedy, bite/meal/burn/cold rules, major images and eventual arcs remain. Some staging and transitions have been changed to repair causality. English production directions; dialogue in spoken Mongolian Cyrillic; Russian lines remain in Russian with Mongolian subtitles. All pacing markers are **editing targets**, not literal scene durations. Dark adult humor, not jokes on a timetable.
>
> **Important dramatic rule:** The shared bowl does *not* infect Ganaa. Under the Bible, infection requires a bite, deep scratch, or infected blood entering a wound. He is bitten later. The bowl is a visual motif of ordinary kindness.


## VO / animation direction legend — read before recording

**Source and scope:** This is the complete Act I v2.0 screenplay with **additional performance and production annotations**. The spoken words and the v2.0 plot are preserved. Scene times (0:45, 3:15, etc.) are *editorial targets*, **not** guaranteed final run times. A final animatic and a native-speaker read are still required for picture lock.

- **[E01-D001]** is an actor/ADR line ID. It is **not spoken** and never appears on screen. The cue following the character name is **playing intention**, not a rigid emotion label; if breath, action or eye line already communicates the moment, prioritize it over the cue.
- **LOW / close** means a private, supported vocal tone, not unintelligible whispering. **WHISPER** means voiced or near-voiceless speech only when the scene's physical danger demands it. **SHOUT** is an action with a destination. Do not yell at maximum intensity on every take.
- **PAUSE** means leave space for a reaction/action, **not** add filler (“өө”, “аа”, unnecessary inner monologue). An ellipsis in original dialogue stays an intentional unfinished thought, not mandatory long silence.
- **OVERLAP** can be used for extras, but named dialogue remains intelligible. Keep slang and profanity as v2.0; a word such as “пизда” is not automatically louder simply because it is a swear.
- **Russian dialogue** stays Russian as written, with the existing Mongolian subtitles. Dasha need not understand Ganaa's Mongolian line; do **not** record the subtitle as extra spoken dialogue.
- **Episode 4** is diegetic Hi8 material. Camera is turned off between segments. No impossible cutaways or clean post-battery recorded audio.
- **Episode 2 ↔ Episode 3:** The repeated Oyun/Lkhagva call is the **same six performances**, edited with two different acoustic perspectives. See [E02-D054–059] / [E03-D013–018].

# EPISODE 1 — АЯГАА ХУВААЛЦ / SHARE THE BOWL

**Day 0 · Late autumn, 1998 · Ganaa**  
**Theme:** A decent thing is done in the wrong place at the wrong time.  
**Sound:** A key dragging against metal; diesel engines; tea splashing in enamel; eventually a baby's cry.

### 0:00 — COLD OPEN

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 0:00 — COLD OPEN**
> **ACTING:** Routine fatigue becomes disbelief. The guard begins irritated; Irina does not panic publicly because she has already seen too much.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Hold a wide locked shot of the freight car longer than comfortable. The sprint is one almost unreadable burst; cut to the fallen key rather than follow the violence. Irina never looks into camera.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Wind through rail gaps; metal key on steel (dry close recording); one wet breath inside the car. Hard cut the scream; record a separate close key-drop. Outside the office, steam hisses steadily.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** Leave 2 beats after Irina stops the recorder. Her silence must carry the implication, not narration.

**EXT. ULAN-UDE FREIGHT YARD — BEFORE DAWN**

A flashlight beam crawls over frost-coated freight cars. Nothing moves. Somewhere, metal scrapes metal.

A GUARD catches the smell, pulls his coat over his nose and knocks with his key.

`[E01-D001]` **ХАРУУЛ** *(Russian; a casual yard call; slightly muffled by scarf, not frightened yet)*: «Эй! Кто там?» [хадмал: Хөөе! Цаана чинь хэн байна?]

He opens the boxcar. Someone stands at the back, barely visible. Breath comes wet and fast.

`[E01-D002]` **ХАРУУЛ** *(Russian; starts annoyed, then trails off as he realizes something is wrong; no final word)*: «Ты чего там?..» [хадмал: Чи тэнд юу хийж байгаа юм?..]

A red figure crosses the freight yard in four seconds. The key lands in a puddle. The scream ends abruptly.

**INT. DISPATCH OFFICE — SAME**

IRINA KOVALEVA, 54, watches from behind a dirty window. She presses RECORD on a small cassette machine. A handwritten page next to her reads: *three minutes? heat?* Several earlier dates are crossed out.

`[E01-D003]` **ИРИНА** *(Russian, recording; clinical and almost flat, but a tiny catch before “почти ровно” betrays fear)*: «Опять. Три минуты... почти ровно.» [хадмал: Дахиад л. Гурван минут... бараг яг.]

Outside, the figure slows, wanders toward the steam pipe and stands against it. Irina turns the recorder off. She stares at the phone with its receiver hanging loose.

### 0:45 — THE ERRAND

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 0:45 — THE ERRAND**
> **ACTING:** Ganaa and the loader share an old, rough friendship. Irina is efficient until she hears Ganaa say he has understood, then nearly begs.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Ganaa works the rope while talking, never posing for a joke. Insert his watch, gift, Irina's cracked satchel clasp and the unread hospital envelope. Ganaa whistles two tuneless notes while checking the tie-down; it stops when Irina approaches.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Diesel idle, canvas flap, ratchet creak, boots on gritty ice. Reduce engine rumble slightly in the Irina exchange so subtitle timings remain readable.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** The smile leaves Irina's face before Ganaa turns away. Give the satchel dropping behind his seat one visible shot for Episode 8 continuity.

**EXT. FREIGHT YARD — DAWN**

GANAA, 46, ties down his cargo. The cabin of his KAMAZ is full of cassette tapes, old cigarette wrappers, a thermos, a child's wrapped present and a family photo stuck behind the sun visor.

His coworker calls from a platform.

`[E01-D004]` **АЧИГЧИН** *(calls over diesel noise with teasing familiarity; grin heard in the voice)*: Ганаа ах аа! Наадах чинь татлагатайгаа явах уу, салхинд алга болох уу?

`[E01-D005]` **ГАНАА** *(throws the comeback over his shoulder; practical, fond, already busy)*: Чиний татсанаас лав дээр байгаа. Нэг үзээд өг л дөө, хө.

`[E01-D006]` **АЧИГЧИН** *(mock-complaining; let the accusation land as an old shared joke)*: Та мөн ч сайхан аргатай хүн шүү. Өөрөө ажил хийдэггүй, хүнийг хийлгэнэ.

`[E01-D007]` **ГАНАА** *(half-laughing, unoffended; the swear is friendly shorthand, not aggression)*: Хуцаад байлгүй нэг татчих.

They laugh. Irina appears with a satchel, the metal clasp held together by wire.

`[E01-D008]` **ИРИНА** *(Russian; quick, low and urgent; she checks whether he is alone before asking)*: «Вы в Наран едете?» [хадмал: Та Наран руу явж байгаа юу?]

`[E01-D009]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian, rough but understandable; functional broken Russian; willing to help, glancing at the birthday gift)*: «Да. Через границу. Наран.» [хадмал: Тийм. Хилээр гараад Наран.]

She shows him an envelope marked for the **Naran public-health duty office**, with instructions to forward the material to the Ministry. There are copies of reports and two cassettes inside. She cannot reach the office by phone.

`[E01-D010]` **ИРИНА** *(Russian; precise, increasingly insistent; stress the distinction between hospital and police)*: «В больницу. Дежурному врачу. Не в милицию. Пожалуйста.» [хадмал: Эмнэлэгт нь. Жижүүр эмчид өг. Цагдаад биш. Гуйж байна.]

`[E01-D011]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian; repeats to confirm he has understood; mild concern about the deadline)*: «Больница... понял. Сегодня?» [хадмал: Эмнэлэг... ойлголоо. Өнөөдөр үү?]

She nods too quickly. He takes it because saying no to a visibly desperate woman feels wrong.

`[E01-D012]` **ГАНАА** *(quietly, Mongolian, as he pockets the envelope; to himself as he pockets the envelope; warm, distracted, not a solemn promise)*: За, эмнэлэг дээр чинь хүргээд өгчихнө өө.

He climbs in. The gift catches his eye. He glances at his watch: he is already late.

`[E01-D013]` **ИРИНА** *(Russian, louder; calls after the moving truck; the first crack of genuine desperation)*: «Не забудьте!» [хадмал: Битгий мартаарай!]

He gives her a thumbs-up; the satchel disappears behind his seat.

### 1:45 — THE PASSENGER

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 1:45 — THE PASSENGER**
> **ACTING:** Hospitality is automatic for Ganaa; Dasha does not try to be ominous. Her speech is minimal because cold, pain and hunger are winning.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Confine eye lines to windshield, enamel cup, bleeding bandage, wrapped birthday meat. The same bowl crosses from Dasha to Ganaa; his unbroken skin stays visible to avoid implying transmission by drinking.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** A bad heater fan, wet cough, enamel-on-metal clink, meat tearing from bone. Let engine noise mask half of Dasha's small movements. Her chewing should be uncomfortable, not a monster sound yet.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** After Dasha ignores “Оставь,” do not add Ganaa commentary. Stay on his uncertain face for an extra breath.

**EXT. BORDER ROAD — AFTERNOON**

Dry yellow grass shows through snow along the ditch. A young woman flags him down: DASHA, 24. Her arm is bundled inside a scarf. The sleeve beneath is stiff with dried blood.

He rolls down his window.

`[E01-D014]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian; short roadside question, spoken through the open window over the engine)*: «Куда?» [хадмал: Хаашаа?]

`[E01-D015]` **ДАША** *(Russian; cold-stiff and economical; guard the wounded arm while answering)*: «До Нарана. К сестре.» [хадмал: Наран хүртэл. Эгч рүүгээ.]

`[E01-D016]` **ГАНАА** *(simple invitation without flirtation; firm on the warning about heat)*: «Садись. Холодно.» [хадмал: Суу. Хүйтэн байна.]

**INT. KAMAZ — MOVING**

The heater wheezes. He unscrews his thermos and pours salty milk tea into the one enamel bowl. He offers it without ceremony.

`[E01-D017]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian, pointing; habitual hospitality, close and low over the heater; hand already offering the bowl)*: «Пей. Горячий.» [хадмал: Уу. Халуун шүү.]

Dasha cups the bowl with shaking hands. She coughs into her sleeve, drinks and passes it back. Ganaa notices a faint bloody smear on the rim. He turns the bowl and drinks from the clean side. No open wound, no infection here.

`[E01-D018]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian, gesturing to her bandage; quiet question, half spoken; eyes on the scarf rather than her face)*: «Рука?..» [хадмал: Гар чинь?..]

`[E01-D019]` **ДАША** *(too quick, too flat; don't decorate the lie)*: «Собака.» [хадмал: Нохой.]

`[E01-D020]` **ГАНАА** *(concern masked by practical questioning; a brief pause before the noun)*: «Больница была?» [хадмал: Эмнэлэгт очсон уу?]

She looks away. Her stomach makes an audible noise.

He reaches for the food wrapped in newspaper: boiled mutton set aside for Altan's birthday. Dasha grabs a piece before he can unfold the paper. She pulls flesh from the bone. It is not appetite anymore. Ganaa watches, first amused, then uncomfortable.

`[E01-D021]` **ГАНАА** *(Mongolian, automatic; automatic comic protest that becomes uneasy by the end; no performed punchline)*: Хөөе, арай үлдээгээрэй. Бүх айлын хоолыг чи ганцаараа идчих нь ээ.

She does not understand the words. He holds up one finger and points at the remaining food.

`[E01-D022]` **ГАНАА** *(Russian; now guarded; gesture does the work, Russian deliberately simple)*: «Оставь. Чуть-чуть.» [хадмал: Жаахныг нь үлдээ.]

Dasha stops chewing. Looks at him. For an instant he thinks she is going to strike him. Then she goes back to the meat, faster.

The radio crackles. The music fades beneath her breathing.

### 3:15 — HÜREL'S CAFÉ

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 3:15 — HÜREL'S CAFÉ**
> **ACTING:** Play the debt banter like men who have said similar things a hundred times. Hürel's first interrupted question is the emotional turning point; after that, no comedy.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Ganaa stays half-turned toward Hürel as he teases, then physically freezes for a full beat at the attack. Stage the bite clearly at the driver's door, then the fuel spill and fire cause-and-effect in three readable shots. Do not show a working truck after the collision.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Oven lid, spoons, card slap, pot steam, two overlapping drivers. Sudden chair scrape kills the warmth. Keep the bite close and short; ignition must follow audible spill and spark, not happen magically.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** Stop café ambience at the sight of Hürel's body; let wind and distant burning take over. Ganaa's question to the livestock driver has no heroic strength.

**EXT./INT. ROADSIDE CAFÉ, OUTSIDE NARAN — LATE AFTERNOON**

A hand-painted sign flickers in the wind. HÜREL, 50s, is cleaning a table with a rag that has seen better days. Two drivers are playing cards. A pot rattles on the stove.

Hürel spots Ganaa through the window.

`[E01-D023]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(shouted from behind the counter in delighted recognition; mock creditor)*: Ганаа ах! Та амьд мэнд үү? Өнгөрсөн удаагийнхаа мөнгийг өгчихөөд бууз идээрэй!

`[E01-D024]` **ГАНАА** *(smiling as he sits; builds a ridiculous defense without waiting for approval)*: Өө, тэр чинь өр биш ээ, Хүрэл ээ. Бид хоёрын хамтын хөрөнгө оруулалт.

`[E01-D025]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(dry arithmetic, delighted to catch him; throw the final phrase away)*: Тэгвэл хөрөнгө оруулалтаа буцааж авъя. Дээр нь хүүтэй нь.

`[E01-D026]` **ГАНАА** *(easy camaraderie, not a threat; curse tossed lightly as he orders)*: Арван бууз өгчих. Тэгээд тооцоогоо хийе, муу лалар аа.

Hürel sees Dasha sitting by the heater, her bandaged arm dangling.

`[E01-D027]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(under his breath; leaning close so the drivers cannot hear; makes mischief without saying it outright)*: Цэцгээ эгч чинь мэдэж байгаа юу?

`[E01-D028]` **ГАНАА** *(immediate, genuinely confused; look at Dasha only after speaking)*: Юуг нь?

`[E01-D029]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(knows exactly what he is implying; pause and turn back to work)*: За, мэдээгүй юм байна.

`[E01-D030]` **ГАНАА** *(brief defensive irritation, not rage; then rushes to justify himself)*: Битгий шаа. Замд даарч явахаар нь суулгаад авсан юм.

`[E01-D031]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(innocent in a deliberately false way; almost to himself)*: Би юу ч хэлээгүй шүү дээ.

A driver chuckles. Ganaa is about to answer when Dasha's chair scrapes the floor.

She bends toward the steam rising from the dumplings, nostrils flaring. Hürel reaches out instinctively to steady her.

`[E01-D032]` **ХҮРЭЛ** *(begins professional concern, cut short by the attack)*: Эгч ээ, зүгээр үү?.. 

His hand comes back red. Dasha has him by the neck. The second driver screams; the other overturns the table. Ganaa freezes one devastating second before moving.

`[E01-D033]` **ГАНАА** *(full raw shout, voice breaks on the name; no explanatory words)*: Хүрэл! ХҮРЭЛ ЭЭ!

He drags a chair into Dasha. She does not feel it. He runs, clutching the keys. Outside, his engine turns over once, twice.

Dasha catches up at the driver's door, snatches his wrist and bites deeply. Ganaa strikes her with the door and slams it shut.

`[E01-D034]` **ГАНАА** *(explodes from pain and panic, mouth close to the steering wheel)*: АСААЧ, ПИЗДА!

The KAMAZ lurches forward, side-swipes the fuel dispenser and jams against the protective concrete block. The nozzle tears free; fuel spreads across the asphalt. Dasha chases a screaming driver toward the fumes. A stray spark ignites the spill.

**Ganaa crawls out the passenger door. The truck is disabled but away from the first wall of flame.** The blaze takes Dasha and the doorway of the café. Ganaa crawls back, tries to reach Hürel, but heat drives him out. Through smoke he sees Hürel lying motionless. He does not rise.

At the edge of the road, an arriving livestock driver pulls up. He sees Ganaa's bitten wrist and bloody jacket.

`[E01-D035]` **ЖОЛООЧ** *(alarms the oncoming driver, shouted through the open cab window)*: Хөөе! Юу болсон бэ?!

`[E01-D036]` **ГАНАА** *(stunned and fragmented; asks for a ride before answering the real question)*: Машин... дэлбэрчихлээ. Наран руу явах уу?

The man looks at the café. Ganaa hides his wrist behind his back.

`[E01-D037]` **ЖОЛООЧ** *(quick, nervous roadside decision; he looks toward the fire, then away)*: Суу, хурдан. Цагдаа дуудахаас өмнө холдоод авъя.

Ganaa climbs in with only the birthday gift. **Irina's satchel remains behind the KAMAZ seat**, intact. The livestock truck pulls away. In the rear-view mirror the café roof smokes. The KAMAZ stands in the yard, scorched, not consumed.

### 4:30 — THE WOUND

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 4:30 — THE WOUND**
> **ACTING:** Ganaa's answer is an emotional shutdown; the livestock driver is a stranger offering ordinary sympathy.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Stay in cramped profile. Move from the red line to the child-size mittens without a reaction close-up; the package trembles in Ganaa's hands.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Road joints, clattering livestock gate, diesel strain; Ganaa rubs dry paper over the gift. Keep the driver's words half-covered by vehicle sounds.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** No inner monologue. Give the pause before “Танина аа” room to hurt.

**INT. LIVESTOCK TRUCK — MOVING**

The driver curses every pothole and talks about the fire. Ganaa barely hears him. He presses his thumb beside the bite. A thread of red is pushing under his skin toward his elbow.

`[E01-D038]` **ЖОЛООЧ** *(tries to be gentle without prying; keep hands on the wheel)*: Хүнд юм тохиолдох нь. Чи нөгөө тогоочийг таньдаг байсан уу?

`[E01-D039]` **ГАНАА** *(one deadened answer, breath shallow; don't sob or explain)*: ...Танина аа.

The driver waits. Ganaa gives him nothing more.

A child-sized pair of mittens peeks from the unwrapped present in his lap. He wraps it again with numb fingers.

### 5:00 — THE DRIVE HOME

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 5:00 — THE DRIVE HOME**
> **ACTING:** The official read is remote and mundane; Ganaa's disbelief leaks out before he remembers there is another person in the cab.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Radio dial, Ganaa's jaw, then sheep moving past the window in real time. Do not cut to imagined meat or show a fantasy. He does not slap or order himself to stop.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Period radio AM crackle, distant sheep bleats, one long horn. Bring in a very low saliva swallow, then let it go. No horror sting under the sheep.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** Allow the driver's “Юу гэнэ ээ?” to puncture the private moment. Hold Ganaa's reflection, not a spoken explanation.

**EXT. OUTSKIRTS OF NARAN — NIGHT**

The livestock truck slows behind sheep crossing the road. The cab radio crackles with an official notice.

`[E01-D040]` **РАДИО** *(impersonal official read, evenly paced; narrow radio bandwidth)*: Эрүүл мэндийн байгууллагаас мэдээлж байна. Ханиад, томуу төст өвчний шинж тэмдэг илэрвэл гэртээ байж, ойр байгаа эмнэлэгт хандана уу. Баталгаагүй мэдээлэлд автахгүй байхыг анхааруулж байна.

`[E01-D041]` **ГАНАА** *(barely audible, not addressing anyone; an angry disbelief escaping under his breath; “Ямар алных нь томуу вэ” rises naturally)*: Томуу гэнэ ээ? Хүрэлийг тэр чинь хазаад... идчихсэн шүү дээ. Ямар алных нь томуу вэ...

The driver turns down the radio and glances at Ganaa.

`[E01-D042]` **ЖОЛООЧ** *(glances across, asking for an explanation but not insisting)*: Юу гэнэ ээ?

`[E01-D043]` **ГАНАА** *(snaps the door shut emotionally; barely looks at him)*: ...Юу ч биш.

A sheep looks in through the glass. Ganaa's eyes follow the pulse in its throat. His hand tightens around the gift. The driver sounds the horn. The sheep scatter. Ganaa keeps staring until the glass reflects his own face.

No commentary. No self-command.

### 6:30 — THE PARTY

> **DIRECTOR / SOUND CARD · E01 · 6:30 — THE PARTY**
> **ACTING:** Play a genuine, affectionate family celebration. Tsetsegmaa's anger is fear for her late husband and child, not a sitcom routine. Sükhee is funny without knowing he is funny. Ganaa's speech gets shorter as the fever advances.
> **BLOCKING / CAMERA:** Place Altan low in frame and often out of adult sightlines; her last “Хамт” has real weight. Let guests talk over one another in background only; keep principal lines intelligible. Show Tsetsegmaa's hand moving to the wrist before she says it. Play chewing close but not grotesquely exaggerated.
> **SOUND / FOLEY:** Crowded concrete apartment: cutlery, kids, kitchen radio, bottle clinks, coat movement. Gradually remove peripheral chatter under Ganaa's breathing; then let all room sounds come rushing back when Altan speaks.
> **SILENCE / EDIT:** The table's silence after Ganaa jerks away must not be filled by a joke. Give his unfinished apology no second attempt.

**INT. GANAA'S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

Forty guests, a tiny apartment, winter coats piled on a bed, wet boots at the entrance. A Soviet radio plays too loudly in the kitchen. Someone is searching for a bottle opener. The birthday candles have already been relit once.

Ganaa comes in, pale, sweating, with one gift and no food.

`[E01-D044]` **ЦЭЦЭГМАА** *(calls above party noise; annoyance softened by relief that he arrived)*: Ганаа! Хаагуур яваад байгаа юм бэ? Алтан чинь чамайг л асуугаад.

`[E01-D045]` **ГАНАА** *(avoids her eyes; reaches for a casual excuse, lets it peter out)*: Замд нэг юм болчихоод... За, ирчихлээ ш дээ.

`[E01-D046]` **ЦЭЦЭГМАА** *(the simplest sharp question; stop him from walking past)*: Юу болсон юм?
