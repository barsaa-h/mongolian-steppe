## Critical unresolved production gates
1. **1998 border and travel:** current real-world road geometry corroborates the long route, but the 1998 seasonal condition, historical border queue and local clocks remain unverified. Draft only.
2. **Fictional infection:** the premise accepts variable incubation; Ganaa's working ~4h25 onset is a story assumption. Approve explicitly for later episodes before locking.
3. **Café disaster blocking:** ignition and fire dynamics need set/VFX review. The cab and Irina's tapes must survive for later recovery.
4. **Apartment camera test:** inward door, sound, stool/bench, routes and infant rescue all require top-down 3D proof. Narrative text alone is not enough.
5. **Voice casting:** no ElevenLabs generation succeeded during this attempt. Need actual audible sample and native Mongolian review.
6. **Length:** 25 minutes remains a target. Dialogue text alone suggests several long action holds and timing pressure in café + ending; the whole cut must be measured.
7. **Episodic cliffhanger:** Ganaa's door failure must be resolved in the first minute of EP02; do not insert a separate new cold open.
