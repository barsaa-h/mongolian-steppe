## Highest-priority read-through risks

- **EP1 15 60-second café:** Heavy 130+ spoken-word exchange plus business with cups and gift. Needs >1 min for restrained, interrupted banter.
- **EP1 26 finale 40 seconds:** Door-thud, Tsetsegmaa warning, child question, Sükhee misunderstanding and held breath compete. Allow more screen time in animatic.
- **EP1 01 first 85 seconds:** Freight horror needs comprehensible geography and off-screen passage of roughly three minutes; do not show an impossible three-minute sprint in 85 seconds of film.
- **EP1 08 border montage:** 50 seconds screen time stands for a 1–2 hour border queue and several hours driving, not a real-time 50-second crossing.
- **EP1 21–26 party:** Onscreen minutes do not equal real elapsed time; show clock/background transition from evening meal to near 21:00 cake.

## Read-through log template
For each scene record: dialogue performance seconds; silent action seconds; travel/environment seconds; total shot estimate; any unmotivated pause; listener clarity and emotional change. Track whether a scene does two jobs. Do not lock until observed durations are documented.

## Recut proposal (NOT applied to subtitle timecodes yet)
Give the café friendship and turn +40–80 extra seconds and the kitchen climax +15–30 seconds **only if the dry read proves they need it**; take time from repetitive driving, explanatory close-ups or duplicated birthday waiting rather than adding 100 filler shots. The 00:00–25:00 stamps remain working slots, not approvals.
