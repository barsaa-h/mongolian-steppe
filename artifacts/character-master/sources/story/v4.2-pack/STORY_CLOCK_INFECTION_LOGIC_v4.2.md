## Fix before script lock

1. **Dasha's border arrival:** Show a departing shared minibus behind her, or one concise plausible visual detail that she had been transported and left roadside. Do not introduce a passport/visa claim not historically researched.
2. **Irina's channels:** Her earlier report/fax (~Day −4) was filed separately; a phone dispatcher is unhelpful today; Ganaa carries a cassette + observations to Naran's duty doctor. Keep the stamped government delay proof for the season.
3. **Café evidence:** Ganaa must visibly *try* to retrieve the bag and be stopped by injury and fire, not simply forget a potentially lifesaving object.
4. **Bite clock:** Current story requires ~4h25 to major loss of control. Variable incubation can permit it, but the series infection rules must explicitly allow this based on bite location and severity. Dasha's own pre-Ganaa bite happens earlier; exact timestamp remains unknown.
5. **Hospital refusal:** Ensure the emotional cause is not only shame: Ganaa rationalizes being late, fears detention, is not thinking clearly due to fever, and knows his daughter is waiting. He should not give a lucid medical analysis after witnessing the café.
6. **1998 season/daylight:** Treat café as fading daylight/dusk and the return journey as night. Verify actual historical date and location-specific lighting in art pass.
7. **Irina evidence vs governor evidence:** tapes detail symptoms only; stamped warning proves official receipt. Neither replaces the other.
