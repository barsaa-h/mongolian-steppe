## The causal chain (the production backbone)

**Freight outbreak → Dasha escaped with bite → Ganaa carries her → café exposure → Ganaa bitten → birthday attack → hospital surge → quarantine confusion and Oyun's locked ward → town emergency + governor misinformation → mistaken school separation → school outbreak → checkpoint crowds + Erdene's hidden patient → flight with teens → herder family seeks missing wife → Dorj risks himself but survives; group loses pack horse and he needs transport → Oyun and newborn reunited with family → van and supplies needed → the group's deliberate café stop → Irina's tapes recovered → governor's stamped delay exposed → survival depends on cooperation at high-pass approach → Sarnai's observations and Oyun's changed triage avert repeat mistake → Tsogt closes the thermal route → shelter with evidence, but no moral neatness.**

## Unresolved by design, not overlooked

1. **World origin:** Irina witnessed earlier freight-yard infections but did not prove the pathogen's ultimate origin. Season 1 doesn't explain the entire pandemic.
2. **Tsetsegmaa and Altan after westbound convoy:** They are alive at the moment they depart EP5. Their further fate is not falsely known.
3. **Exactly when the storm hits:** Forecast ranges are dramatic pressure. Fix one forecast timetable and verify it across EP7–10 during screenplay lock.
4. **Exact hospital-floor and school-yard plans:** These require drawn sets to prevent physically impossible escapes.
5. **Exact Hi8 model / battery and tape behavior:** prop research before locked “night vision” storyboard.

## Quality thresholds for an episode draft

- A scene must do **two jobs**: advance a concrete need, reveal a person, or change the danger. Scenic zombies alone are insufficient.
- A joke must belong to one specific speaker, with a relationship or real grievance behind it.
- If an ordinary person would run, call for help, use a nearby door, or wait, show why they cannot—or let them do it.
- No character describes their own thoughts for the viewer. Exceptions require a compelling reason (Anu choosing to record a confession, an operational call, a real attempt to persuade).
- Silence should alter the choice or increase dread, not fill a quota.
- No unexplained jump in miles, weather, wounds, weapons, baby formula, camera battery, fuel or officials' knowledge.
- Horrific deaths are consequences rather than punishments assigned to “nice” or “bad” characters.
- Every ending provides **one settled emotional change** and **one open concrete pressure**. Don't end each installment with a random new bite.

**Additional hard lock:** Narantsetseg remains inside the locked maternity wing after turning. Ep 7 must show Tsogt seeing her through wired glass, not having her inexplicably appear outside in the boiler-house car park.

**Departure for dramatic weight:** Dorj survives the EP6 diversion in v3. An elder should not automatically die in the episode that introduces him. His injury motivates the UAZ rescue, and his survival sharpens Tsogt's loss in the finale.

**Logistical fix:** The governor does not jump from Day 1 evacuation straight to a Day 7 reunion near the pass. His convoy loses several days at a failed southern military-transfer point, then heads for the same weather relay after the broadcast. Track fuel and passenger roster.
