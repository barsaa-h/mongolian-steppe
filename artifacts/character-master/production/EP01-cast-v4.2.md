## Differences and uncertainties

- Nyamka, Bataa and Togoo are all explicitly named and speak in v4.2. Pasha and Tungalag now have names; preserve the old actor IDs. The pharmacy is absent. The old café driver is not explicitly established as a separate person: the second driver is the one explicitly carrying a plate and escaping. The old `hospital-guard` is not identified as the orderly; a separate draft avoids silently equating them.
- Hürel's helper is addressed but neither seen nor given dialogue. The status cell above uses the available `voice only` category for an unshown role; this is not evidence that the helper's voice is heard. Scene 03's radio has no separate manifest cue; the PA advisory in 09 is described, with no named speaker or manifest cue. Other incidental laughter and unnamed groups are not separate scripted identities.
- v4.0's claims that Hürel is killed and Dasha dies at the pump go beyond v4.2: Hürel does not emerge, and Dasha disappears behind smoke. The v4.2 script does not confirm either fate. The v4.2 guard's ultimate fate is also not explicitly confirmed.
- Drafting uses fictional art palettes, not ancestry measurements. Pasha's Russian casting palette is an artistic choice; Russian is a script fact. Actor folders predate these DNA changes and require rebuilds to reflect them.
