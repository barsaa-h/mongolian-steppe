# Reference image prompts

Use these with any image generator. Every prompt describes a **fictional** person; never ask for a real
or famous person's likeness. Generate 2–4 images per prompt and keep the ones that look most like real,
ordinary people.

**Where to put the results:** `artifacts/character-master/references/<id>/`, for example
`references/hurel/hurel-1.png`. Base-model variety sheets go in `references/base-m/`, `base-f/`,
`base-c/`. Generated references are art direction only, never textures or likenesses to copy.

## Shared ending (paste after every prompt)

> Photorealistic character reference sheet, three views of the same person side by side: front view,
> three-quarter view, side profile. Head and shoulders. Neutral relaxed expression, mouth closed, eyes
> open looking straight ahead. Even soft studio lighting, plain light-grey background. Natural skin with
> visible pores and real imperfections, no retouching, no beauty filter, no stylization, no cartoon.
> Ordinary-looking person, not a model or celebrity. Set in Mongolia in 1998.

If the generator mixes up the three views, generate the front view and the side profile separately.

## Base model variety sheets (most important: do these first)

**base-m.** A grid of 8 different ordinary Mongolian men, ages 18, 25, 35, 45, 55, 65, 75 and a heavy
40-year-old, each face front view, neutral expression, even studio light, plain grey background,
photorealistic, natural skin, 1998 Mongolia, clearly different individuals with different eyelid
shapes, noses, cheekbones and face shapes.

**base-f.** The same as base-m but 8 different ordinary Mongolian women, ages 16, 24, 30, 38, 50, 60, 70
and a heavier 45-year-old.

**base-c.** A grid of 6 different ordinary Mongolian children: girls aged 3, 6 and 10, boys aged 4, 8 and
12, front view, neutral expression, even studio light, plain grey background, photorealistic, natural
child skin, winter-reddened cheeks on some.

**base-profiles.** Side profiles only of 6 different Mongolian adults (3 men, 3 women, ages 25–65),
plain grey background, photorealistic. This shows nose, brow and chin structure.

## Episode 1 characters

**hurel.** A 55-year-old Mongolian man, roadside café cook. Heavyset with a round full face that is
still natural, not swollen. Shiny steam-flushed skin, a sparse moustache, thinning short black hair
with some grey, warm tired eyes, one gold crown on an upper canine visible only if the lips part.
Wears a stained white cook's apron over a faded shirt.

**dasha.** A 24-year-old Russian woman, a hitchhiker. Thin, gaunt cheeks, pale skin with a feverish
flush on the cheeks, dark circles under the eyes, cracked lips, greasy light-brown hair pulled back,
frightened guarded eyes. Wears a worn winter coat and a dirty scarf.

**irina.** A 54-year-old Russian woman epidemiologist. Long lean face, deep-set grey eyes, a deep frown
line between the brows, smoker's lines around the lips, short practical greying hair, a slight red
mark on the bridge of the nose from glasses. Wears a dark wool coat. Sharp, exhausted, urgent.

**tsetsegmaa.** A 30-year-old Mongolian woman, a wife and mother dressed for her daughter's birthday
party. Natural pretty-but-ordinary face, high cheekbones, light dark-red lipstick, a 1990s perm, slight
tiredness under the eyes, one small mole on the cheek. Warm and observant. Wears a party blouse.

**dulmaa.** A 65-year-old Mongolian grandmother. Deeply weathered sun-darkened skin, many wrinkles, age
spots, grey hair in a bun under a headscarf, sharp narrow eyes, one gold tooth. Wears a traditional
deel. Traditional and observant.

**sukhee.** A 50-year-old Mongolian man, a drunk neighbour at a party. Red nose with broken capillaries,
puffy eyelids, unshaven stubble, a messy comb-over, a loose grin. Wears a cheap 1990s suit jacket over
a sweater.

**altan.** A 3-year-old Mongolian girl at her birthday. Round toddler face, rosy winter cheeks, fine
wispy black hair in two tiny pigtails with ribbons, a small button nose, shy sleepy eyes. Wears a paper
crown and a birthday dress.

**yard-guard.** A 55-year-old Russian-speaking railway freight-yard guard in Siberia, Buryat-looking.
Grey stubble, broken capillaries on the cheeks from cold, heavy bags under the eyes, flattened hair
from a winter hat. Wears a guard's padded jacket. Bored, cold, tired.

**yard-runner.** A 35-year-old wiry Mongolian labourer with a bony long face, an angular jaw and a
humped nose, chapped frost-damaged skin. Wears dirty work clothes.

**party-young-man.** A 22-year-old Mongolian man at a family party with a buzz cut and faint acne
scars, a heart-shaped face, a shy smile. Wears a sweater.

**party-young-woman.** A 23-year-old Mongolian woman at a family party with a 1990s perm, light
lipstick and a soft round face. Wears a blouse.

**party-older-man.** A 69-year-old Mongolian man at a family party with a long lean face, hollow
cheeks, a long curved nose, short white hair and a gold tooth. Wears a traditional deel.
