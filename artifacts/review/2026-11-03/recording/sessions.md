# EP01 recording session plan

Ten sessions. One voice family per session, calm material first, the screaming
session last. Total spoken time on paper is **710.8 s** (about 12 minutes);
booked studio time is **365 minutes** (about 6 hours), because a usable take
includes setup, a listen-back, and at least one honest retake.

## Why this order

- **By character first.** Changing voice every line is slower and less accurate
  than staying in one placement for a block. It also makes it obvious when a
  take has drifted into the wrong person. Within every session, one character's
  cues are contiguous; the short roles share a session because setup costs more
  than their lines do.
- **Calm before loud.** Every session starts with the material that needs
  precision, not volume. The voice is freshest at the start; the screams are at
  the end of the last session, not in the middle of a working day.
- **The final session is the exception.** It is ordered by intensity rather than
  by character, because its whole job is to put the quiet ending first and the
  B17 screams last.
- **Small roles before the big ones.** Sessions 1, 2, 4, 5 and 9 establish the
  room, the format and the supporting cast before the two long Ganaa sessions.
  If the setup is wrong, it costs a short session, not a long one.
- **The ending is its own session.** B23–B26 are quiet but emotionally
  expensive, and B16–B18 are the only genuinely loud material. They share the
  final session so the rest of the episode is already in the can when the voice
  is tired.

If a session runs long, stop at a cue boundary and continue the next day. Do
not record a scream session in two halves unless the second half is another
calm day; a tired voice cannot fake a scream.

## Before every session (5–8 minutes)

1. Quiet the room, phone in airplane mode, fridge/fan off.
2. Mic at 15–20 cm, off-axis, sock pop filter.
3. Set gain on ordinary speech, peak about −12 dBFS.
4. Record 2–3 s of room tone to `audio/vo/roomtone/session-0X.wav`.
5. Read the reference line from `characters.md` out loud twice.
6. Open the session sheet. Read the previous line for the first cue.

## Session 01 — Irina and the telephone (25 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-004`, `005`, `007`, `008`, `044`, `046`, `048`, `050`,
`052`, `006`.

**Order:** dictaphone note → phone call → morning follow-up → envelope →
truck window → the raised «Сего́дня!» → the dispatcher. The dispatcher line
(`006`) is last because it is a different voice and a different distance. The
only raised line is `052`, just before it.

**Notes:** this is the calibration session. Do not rush it to "get to the real
acting". If the room is wrong here, it is wrong for 197 cues. Irina is native
Russian and controlled; the dispatcher is distant and bored, recorded a little
further from the mic or filtered later, never shouted.

## Session 02 — Dasha and Tungalag (25 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-074`, `078`, `079`, `081`, `083`, `088`, `092`, `032`,
`065`.

**Order:** Dasha's short answers first, then Tungalag's domestic lines. Dasha
is quiet and clipped; Tungalag is warmer and slower. Keep them clearly
different women.

**Notes:** Dasha's lines are short but they carry the turn of the episode. Do
not make her fragile-cute. Record her close and soft; the truck cab is small.
Tungalag is the young mother with the infant; she is not a second Tsetsegmaa.

## Session 03 — Tsetsegmaa: the day before (40 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-017`, `019`, `021`, `022`, `027`, `029`, `031`, `034`,
`039`, `057`, `064`, `066`, `069`, `071`, `095`, `097`, `153`, `155`, `160`,
`162`, `164`, `170`, `172`.

**Order:** shop and kitchen calm → the child at the door → the coal-dust
conversation → the party argument. The B21–B22 cues are last because they are
the only place her anger is close to the surface.

**Notes:** the first half is practical and quick. The second half is the same
woman with less patience. Do not play the ending in the shop scenes; the
audience should notice the change only when Ganaa comes home. Take a 5-minute
break after `EP01-VO-097`, before the party block.

## Session 04 — Dulmaa and the older women (35 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-030`, `033`, `063`, `067`, `016`, `018`, `020`, `026`,
`013`, `015`, `036`, `038`, `056`, `152`, `165`, `173`.

**Order:** Dulmaa's kitchen and party lines → Nyamka's shop → the bread seller
→ the two neighbours → the nurse → back to Dulmaa for the bandage and the
candles.

**Notes:** Dulmaa is the anchor. Find her once — lower than Tsetsegmaa, slower,
warm but not soft — and then make the shopkeeper, seller and neighbours as
small deliberate shifts. Do not use the same voice for Nyamka and the seller;
Nyamka is brisk, the seller is slow. The nurse is clinical, not warm.

## Session 05 — Altan (20 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-035`, `037`, `068`, `070`, `072`, `093`, `094`, `096`.

**Order:** the door and the disappointment → the song and the food → the other
truck. All quiet. No party shouts in this session; they are in session 10.

**Notes:** record close, soft, with a small mouth. Let the child be literal.
Take a break after every four cues; a child voice is harder to hold than an
adult one. Do not pitch-shift by default. If you must, follow the exact
commands and limits in `characters.md` and keep the unshifted take.

## Session 06 — Ganaa I: the road and the border (40 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-041`, `043`, `045`, `047`, `049`, `051`, `054`, `055`,
`073`, `075`, `076`, `077`, `080`, `082`, `084`, `085`, `086`, `087`, `089`,
`090`, `091`.

**Order:** Pasha and the strap → Irina at the barrier → the border officer →
the road with Dasha. Keep the Russian practical and accented; the Mongolian
curse (`055`) is the first crack in the day.

**Notes:** this is where Ganaa's voice is established for the whole episode.
Do not make the Russian too good; he is a Mongolian driver who uses Russian for
work. The Dasha scenes are quiet and close — do not project into the cab.
Take a break after `EP01-VO-077`, before the photograph.

## Session 07 — Ganaa II: café and aftermath (45 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-102`, `104`, `106`, `108`, `111`, `113`, `115`, `117`,
`147`, `149`, `151`, `154`, `156`, `158`, `161`, `163`, `166`, `168`, `171`,
`142`, `144`.

**Order:** café banter with Hürel first, then the road-side exit and the party
questions, then the protected B19 line last. The protected line is the most
important take in the episode; do it while the voice is warm, not after an hour
of shouting.

**Notes:** the café block is verbal sparring, not comedy. Keep it fast and dry.
The party block is a man lying badly while his hand throbs. The protected line must be exact: «Томуу гэнэ ээ? Хүрэлийг тэр чинь хазаад... идчихсэн шүү дээ. Ямар алных нь томуу вэ...». Record it twice if needed, but do not rewrite it.

## Session 08 — Hürel, Bataa, Togoo and Sükhee (45 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-101`, `103`, `105`, `107`, `109`, `110`, `112`, `114`,
`116`, `118`, `023`, `025`, `058`, `061`, `098`, `024`, `028`, `059`, `062`,
`099`, `157`, `159`, `167`, `169`.

**Order:** Hürel's café block → Bataa → Togoo → Sükhee. Save the drunk relative
for the end; he is the most energetic supporting voice.

**Notes:** Hürel and Ganaa must not blur. Hürel is warmer, a little lighter,
and he smiles while he talks. Bataa and Togoo bicker like men who have done it
for years; do not make either stupid. Sükhee is loud and tactless but every
word is clear. His B26 lines are in the final session.

## Session 09 — One-offs: yard, bus, road and radio (40 min)

**Cues:** `EP01-VO-001`, `002`, `003`, `010`, `012`, `014`, `053`, `060`,
`100`, `143`, `145`, `146`, `148`, `150`, `040`, `042`, `141`, `009`, `011`.

**Order:** yard guard → passenger and boy → border officer → the truck push →
coal driver → livestock driver's calm lines → Pasha → radio bulletin → bus
driver's loud lines last.

**Notes:** the point of this session is separation. The yard guard is Russian
and cold; Pasha is Russian and warm; the border officer is Russian and tired.
The bus driver and the livestock driver are both outdoor Mongolian men but the
bus driver is clipped and the livestock driver is humane. The radio announcer
is flat and institutional — no character, no drama. The livestock driver's B18
shouts are in session 10; here he is calm.

## Session 10 — Final: strain, screams and the ending (50 min)

**Cues, in order:** `EP01-VO-174`–`197` (B23–B26, quiet ending first), then
`EP01-VO-119`–`129` (B16, café confrontation), then `EP01-VO-135`–`140` (B18,
fire), then `EP01-VO-130`, `131`, `133`, `132`, `134` (B17, screams last).

Exact cue ids:

```
B23: 174 175 176 177 178 179 180 181 182 183
B24: 184 185 186 187
B25: 188 189 190 191 192
B26: 193 194 195 196 197
B16: 119 120 121 122 123 124 125 126 127 128 129
B18: 135 136 137 138 139 140
B17: 130 131 133 132 134
```

**Order and rests:**

1. B23–B25: the song, the mittens, the last almost-normal minute. Quiet, close,
   take your time. These are emotionally heavy but vocally cheap.
2. Rest 5 minutes.
3. B26: the door handle, Tsetsegmaa's shouts, Altan's last question. Two or
   three raised lines; not screams yet.
4. Rest 10 minutes. Water. No throat clearing.
5. B16: the café confrontation. Hürel's questions, Ganaa's «Битгий шаа», the
   first alarm. Keep it verbal, not shouty.
6. B18: the fire, the livestock driver, Ganaa's broken sentences. Ganaa is
   breathless, not loud.
7. B17, last: `130` «Хүрэл! ХҮРЭЛ ЭЭ!», `131` the driver, `133` «Асаа...
   асаач!», `132` «ГАР! Гараач, пизда!», `134` «ПИЗДА! АСААЧ ДЭЭ!». These are
   the only full shouts in the episode. Do them once or twice, not ten times.

**Vocal safety for the scream block:**

- Warm up for ten minutes before B17, not two. Hum, lip trills, gentle sirens.
- A recorded shout is **supported breath through an open throat**, not a
  scrape. If you feel gravel or a tickle, stop.
- Do not whisper-scream. Whispering a scream is harder on the folds than
  shouting.
- Step back 5–10 cm for the shouts and keep the gain where it was. Do not lean
  into the mic; the pop filter and the distance are doing the work.
- Keep each shout short. The lines are 0.5–1.3 seconds; do not extend them for
  drama.
- No throat clearing between takes. Swallow, sip water, wait.
- If the voice is hoarse after `134`, stop the session. Do not "fix it
  tomorrow" by recording the screams again the same day.
- The next day, no whispering and no smoking; give the voice 24 hours before
  the next loud session.

## After every session (10 minutes)

1. Run `node episodes/EP01/threejs/tools/vo-check.mjs`.
2. Read `out/vo-report.md`. Fix blockers now; note warnings for the next pass.
3. Back up `audio/vo/` off the laptop.
4. Tick the session off in this file and write the date.
5. If you used a `_t2`, decide now which file is primary and rename it.
