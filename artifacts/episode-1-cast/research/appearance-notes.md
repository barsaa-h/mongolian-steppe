# Episode 1 appearance research

Reviewed the complete available cleaned dialogue master (977 lines, Episodes
1–5) and series bible v0.2 (250 lines). Local copies exactly match the same
Downloads sources by SHA-256. The bible title says 15 episodes, but its detailed
outline ends at Episode 10; no missing later script was invented.

## Evidence used

- Jim Anderson, [World Bank, photographs from the 1990s and 2015](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/eastasiapacific/dynamic-ulaanbaatarphotographs-from-1990s-and-the-present), and the photographer's [1994/1997 high-resolution market photographs](https://jimnosredna.com/2019/01/05/zooming-in-on-ulaanbaatars-black-market-high-resolution-photographs/).
  Primary period evidence for a mixture of everyday jackets, knitwear, trousers,
  caps and traditional clothes. The author describes photographing truck drivers
  during his 1997 work. Reference only; these photographs are not texture sources.
- Anthropologist Chris Kaplonski's [Changing Ulaanbaatar](https://www.chriskaplonski.com/ub.html).
  First-hand early-1990s city photographs; supports the same period rather than
  substituting modern fashion photography.
- [WHO/Mongolian STEPS 2006 report](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/ncds/ncd-surveillance/data-reporting/mongolia/steps/december-2006-mongolia-steps-survey.pdf).
  Measurements vary by sex, age and locality. The 15–24 cohort is reported as
  urban male 168.3 cm/female 159.5 cm and rural male 166.1 cm/female 157.0 cm.
  This is 2006 evidence, not a measured 1998 cast or a single universal mean.
  Heights for fictional individuals are artistic choices around a plausible range.
- Batsevich, Yasina and Sukhova, [1986–1990 rural Mongolian sample](https://archaeology.nsc.ru/en/publications/jr-aeae-en/18-46-1/annot-16/), DOI 10.17746/1563-0110.2018.46.1.144-153.
  The research covers 970 adults aged 18–80 across five populations, illustrating
  why one generic face/body is insufficient. It does not provide individual cast
  measurements or justify reading ancestry from a face.
- [UNESCO, Culture of the Mongol Deel](https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-2200).
  The garment ensemble includes the long deel, sash/belt, hat and boots, with
  distinct styles and use cases. Useful for Dulmaa's costume; the entire town
  should not be dressed in ceremonial deels.

## Artistic application

Create individuals: different head outlines, cheek volume, nose bridges, chin
width, eyelid shapes, body weight, hairlines and posture. Use warm-to-neutral
skin variation and dark hair for the Mongolian casting, with individual variation.
These are fictional designs informed by references, not scans or ethnic averages.
Wrinkles, pores and pimples are human age/life details, not ethnic markers.

Ganaa (46): stockier trucker, receding hair, mild stubble, crow's feet, forehead
and mouth creases, restrained wind redness. Dulmaa (65): aged anatomy, deeper
creases and pigment variation, grey hair, everyday deel. Tsetsegmaa (30): adult
face with mild under-eye texture, not a teenager or a glamour model. Hürel (50s):
fuller cook, worn apron, heat-weathered face. Sükhee: fuller neighbour, ordinary
jacket, imperfect skin; no invented exact age is treated as canon.

Irina (54) and Dasha (24) have Russian dialogue; Dasha is called Russian in the
existing roster. Their Slavic casting is a design choice, not an inference that
language fixes ancestry. The freight guard's ethnicity is unstated; his casting
is explicitly a production choice. The opening runner has no named identity.

Three anonymous birthday guests provide young-adult variation and restrained
acne/healed blemishes. They are background designs, not invented speaking roles.
Altan is a three-year-old with toddler geometry, a modest birthday outfit and
paper crown. Do not use adult makeup, stubble, age creases or acne on her.
The off-screen crying baby has no body model and is not Naran (born in Ep 2).

## Face revision after cast review

The user requested more distinct East Asian faces and specifically retained
Irina and Dasha's earlier Russian casting. The unnamed runner now has East Asian
appearance as a production choice; no canonical ancestry or identity is assigned.
Each recipe's `faceDesign` describes its individual silhouette, eyelids, bridge,
cheek volume and mouth. Stronger contrasts include Ganaa's broad square jaw,
Hurel's short full face, Sukhee's longer projecting chin, Tsetsegmaa's high
cheekbones and tapered jaw, and the young female guest's rounded cheeks.
The toddler keeps age-appropriate proportions and no age creases or acne.

This revision also replaces coarse eyes and removes the visible stock lash cards
that produced mascara-like spikes. Iris saturation and surface roughness are
corrected for the actual Blender and GLB renders. These are direct mesh/material
changes, not a portrait filter or copied photographic likeness.
